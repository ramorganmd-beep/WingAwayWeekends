Laurel Highlands

Fallingwater, a night in a Wright house and mountain resorts.

The Laurel Highlands hold two of Frank Lloyd Wright’s most famous houses. Tour Fallingwater and Kentuck Knob, then spend the night in a Wright-designed house at Polymath Park, or splurge at Nemacolin, a five-star resort with a spa. Hike the falls at Ohiopyle and stroll the shops of Ligonier.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Latrobe.

KLBE, Arnold Palmer Regional, is a towered field with an 8,200-foot runway, about 20 minutes from Ligonier and an hour from Fallingwater and Nemacolin.

The FBO

Laurel Highlands Jet Center

Arnold Palmer Regional Airport, Latrobe, PA

724-539-4533

Confirm FBO hours, fees and ground transportation. No live fuel price is shown.

Appalachian weather: valley fog in the mornings, ridge-top turbulence, summer thunderstorms, and low ceilings and icing in winter. About 400 nm from Myrtle Beach.

Getting around

Rent a car.

The houses, parks and resorts are spread out; a car is essential.
