Islamorada

Turquoise water, tarpon and sunsets in the sand.

Islamorada, in the upper Florida Keys, calls itself the sport-fishing capital of the world. Stay at a relaxed oceanfront resort, hand-feed giant tarpon at Robbie’s, snorkel the reef, and watch the sunset from a table in the sand on Florida Bay. It is quieter and greener than Key West.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in Marathon, 45 minutes south.

KMTH, Florida Keys Marathon International, is the closest public airport to Islamorada, about 45 minutes north up the Overseas Highway.

The FBO

Million Air Florida Keys

9400 Overseas Highway, Marathon, FL 33050

305-743-1995

Listed hours are 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., with an after-hours call-out fee; sources give slightly different street numbers, so confirm. Arrange a rental car ahead. No live fuel price is shown.

The route crosses open water and military airspace near Key West and Homestead. Summer brings afternoon storms; hurricane season runs June to November. Review the sectional, restricted areas and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

You’ll want a car.

The Overseas Highway links everything. Allow extra time; traffic can be slow on weekends.
