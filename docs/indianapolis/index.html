Indianapolis

Racing history, art and a lively downtown.

Indianapolis is easy, friendly and full of surprises. Tour the Indianapolis Motor Speedway museum and track, bike the Cultural Trail through downtown, wander the gardens and art at Newfields, have shrimp cocktail at St. Elmo and stay in a restored Coca-Cola bottling plant.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Eagle Creek.

KEYE, Eagle Creek Airpark, is a non-towered GA field on the northwest side, about 20 minutes from downtown and close to the Speedway.

The FBO

Jet Access

Eagle Creek Airpark, Indianapolis, IN

317-293-6935

Confirm FBO hours, fees and ground transportation. No live fuel price is shown.

Ohio Valley weather: summer thunderstorms and haze, low ceilings and icing from late fall through winter. About 510 nm from Myrtle Beach. A long nonstop leg or a fuel stop.
