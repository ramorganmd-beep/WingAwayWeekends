Victoria, BC

Gardens, whales and the Empress.

Victoria is British Columbia’s capital, a harbor city at the southern tip of Vancouver Island with Victorian buildings, gardens and mild weather. It is a short hop from the San Juan Islands. Land at Victoria International and clear customs (or land a floatplane in the Inner Harbour), stay at the Fairmont Empress, have afternoon tea, spend a day at Butchart Gardens, take a whale-watching trip, and eat fish and chips on the harbor at Red Fish Blue Fish.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Victoria International.

CYYJ, Victoria International, is a towered Canadian airport of entry with long runways, about 30 minutes from downtown and 15 minutes from Butchart Gardens. Floatplanes can use the Inner Harbour water aerodrome (CYWH) downtown.

The FBO

YYJ FBO Services (Shell Aerocentre)

Victoria International Airport, Sidney, BC

250-655-5346

The Shell Aerocentre sells jet fuel but reportedly not avgas; the Victoria Flying Club (250-656-2833) has self-serve 100LL. Confirm customs arrangements with CBSA before you go. For the return, Friday Harbor (KFHR) and Bellingham (KBLI) have U.S. customs; confirm hours and call ahead. Crossing the border: see the Flying to Canada checklist below.

Pacific Northwest flying: marine stratus and low ceilings much of the year, rain and icing in the mountains from fall to spring, and clear but busy summer skies over the Strait of Juan de Fuca. Carry flotation for the water crossings. About 2160 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Taxi, car or seaplane.

Rent a car or take a taxi downtown; the Inner Harbour is walkable.

Crossing the border

Flying to Canada

CYYJ is a Canadian airport of entry. A trip to Canada takes a little paperwork each way; here is the checklist.

Before you go
  • Passports for everyone aboard, plus your pilot certificate and medical.
  • Radio paperwork: an FCC Restricted Radiotelephone Operator Permit for the pilot and an aircraft radio station license for international flight.
  • Insurance: proof of liability insurance that meets Canadian requirements, carried on board.
  • CBP user-fee decal on the aircraft for the return to the U.S.
  • Flight plan: file one for the border crossing and keep your transponder on with an assigned code.
Flying into Canada
  • File eAPIS at least 60 minutes before leaving the U.S.
  • Call CANPASS at 1-888-226-7277 2 to 48 hours before you arrive, and land at CYYJ within your stated arrival window.
  • After landing, follow the CBSA instructions you were given; stay with the aircraft until you are cleared.
Coming home to the U.S.
  • File eAPIS again at least 60 minutes before leaving Canada.
  • Land first at a U.S. airport with customs, such as Friday Harbor (KFHR) or Bellingham (KBLI). Confirm CBP hours and any advance-notice call before you go.
  • Fuel in Canada is sold by the liter in Canadian dollars, plus taxes.

Rules change; check the current requirements before every trip: AOPA’s Canada guide · CBSA CANPASS · CBP eAPIS
