Georgia · Couples · Two nights

Golden Isles

Live oaks, marshes and three barrier islands.

Georgia’s Golden Isles are a quieter, older-feeling coast, with live oaks draped in Spanish moss and wide salt marshes. Land right on St. Simons Island, bike to the 1872 lighthouse, walk Driftwood Beach on Jekyll Island, stay at the Gilded Age Jekyll Island Club or the Cloister at Sea Island and eat shrimp fresh from the boats.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on the island.

KSSI, McKinnon St. Simons Island Airport, is a non-towered field on the island, a few minutes from the village, the pier and the lighthouse.

The FBO

Velocity FBO St. Simons Island

115 Terminal Way, St. Simons Island, GA 31522

912-638-8617

The FBO lists daily hours of 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. Confirm fees, cars and parking. No live fuel price is shown.

A short flight of about 200 nm. Summer brings afternoon storms, and hurricane season runs June to November.

Getting around

Bikes and a car.

St. Simons is great by bike; a car covers Jekyll Island and Sea Island.
