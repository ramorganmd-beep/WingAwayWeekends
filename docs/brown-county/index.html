Brown County & Bloomington

An artists’ village in the wooded hills of southern Indiana.

Brown County has drawn painters for more than a century. Browse the galleries and shops of Nashville, drive the ridges of Brown County State Park in fall color, visit T.C. Steele’s home and studio, taste wine, and stay at a 19th-century country inn or the park’s lodge. Bloomington, home of Indiana University, is 20 minutes away for dinner.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Bloomington.

KBMG, Monroe County Airport, is a towered field about 30 minutes from Nashville and 10 minutes from Bloomington.

The FBO

Cook Aviation

Monroe County Airport, Bloomington, IN

812-825-2392

Confirm FBO hours, fees and ground transportation. No live fuel price is shown.

Ohio Valley weather: summer thunderstorms and haze, low ceilings and icing from late fall through winter. About 500 nm from Myrtle Beach. A long nonstop leg or a fuel stop.

Seaplane access

Lake Monroe (07I), public-use seaplane landing area

Bloomington, IN · Indiana DNR 317-232-0935

317-232-0935

Water lanes: SW/NE 15,000 × 4,000 ft, about 12 nm from Nashville.

A lake use permit is required: call 812-837-9546. No seaplane operations between sunset and sunrise. Low-altitude, high-speed military training over or near the reservoir. (FAA chart supplement remarks.)

You will need a ride from the lake.

Water landing rules, conditions and docking change; call the base and check the chart supplement before you go.

A car connects Bloomington, Nashville and the park; Nashville itself is walkable.
