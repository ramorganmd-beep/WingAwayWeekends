Ocean City & Berlin

A classic boardwalk, bayside sunsets and wild horses.

Ocean City is Maryland’s classic beach town, with a three-mile boardwalk, Thrasher’s fries and sunsets over the bay. Pair it with a night in Berlin, a small historic town a few miles inland, and a morning at Assateague Island, where wild horses roam the dunes.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land by the bay.

KOXB, Ocean City Municipal Airport, is a non-towered field on the mainland a few minutes from the beach and about 10 minutes from Berlin.

The FBO

Ocean City Municipal Airport

Ocean City Municipal Airport, Berlin, MD

410-213-2471

The airport runs its own fuel and services. Confirm hours, fees and rental cars. No live fuel price is shown.

Summer weekends are busy with banner-tow and sightseeing traffic. About 335 nm from Myrtle Beach.

Getting around

A car for the coast.

The boardwalk is walkable, and a bus runs along Coastal Highway; a car covers Berlin and Assateague.
