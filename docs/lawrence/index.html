Lawrence

A lively college town on Mass Street.

Lawrence is one of the Plains’ most charming small cities, with a lively main street and deep history from the Free State struggle. Stay at the historic Eldridge Hotel, stroll Massachusetts Street, have a beer at Free State Brewing, see the Spencer Museum of Art and catch a film or show at Liberty Hall.

Land at Lawrence.

KLWC, Lawrence Municipal, is a non-towered field across the river, about 10 minutes from downtown.
