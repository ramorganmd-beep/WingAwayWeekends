Madison

A capital between two lakes, with a famous farmers’ market.

Madison sits on a narrow isthmus between two lakes, with the Capitol at its center. Stay at The Edgewater on Lake Mendota, browse the country’s largest producer-only farmers’ market around the Capitol on Saturday, have a beer on the Memorial Union Terrace at sunset, dine at L’Etoile and drive to Frank Lloyd Wright’s Taliesin.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Madison.

KMSN, Dane County Regional, is a towered Class C airport about 10 minutes from the Capitol. Wisconsin Aviation’s terminal has the Jet Room restaurant.

The FBO

Wisconsin Aviation

Dane County Regional Airport, Madison, WI

Great Lakes weather: lake-effect clouds and snow from late fall through winter, summer thunderstorms and fog over cold water in spring. About 750 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Walk the isthmus.

Downtown, the Capitol and the campus are walkable; a car covers Taliesin.
