Natchitoches

The oldest town in the Louisiana Purchase, on Cane River Lake.

Founded in 1714, Natchitoches (say “NACK-a-tish”) is older than New Orleans. Stay in a historic B&B, stroll brick-paved Front Street above Cane River Lake, eat a Natchitoches meat pie, visit the Creole plantations of the Cane River Creole National Historical Park and see the homes made famous by Steel Magnolias.

Land at the regional airport.

KIER, Natchitoches Regional Airport, is a non-towered field a few minutes from downtown.
