Birmingham

Award-winning dining, civil rights history and a flight museum by the runway.

Birmingham has one of the South’s best restaurant scenes and some of its most important history. Walk the Civil Rights District and the 16th Street Baptist Church, visit the Southern Museum of Flight next to the airport, see the world’s largest motorcycle collection at Barber, watch the sunset from Vulcan Park and book dinner at Bottega or Automatic Seafood.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Birmingham.

KBHM, Birmingham–Shuttlesworth International, is a towered airport about 10 minutes from downtown. The Southern Museum of Flight is beside the field.

The FBO

Atlantic Aviation

Birmingham–Shuttlesworth International Airport, Birmingham, AL

205-849-5520

Million Air Birmingham is a second FBO on the field. Confirm hours, fees and rental cars. No live fuel price is shown.

About 390 nm from Myrtle Beach. Summer brings afternoon storms.

Getting around

Rent a car.

Downtown is compact, but Barber, Vulcan and Mountain Brook need a car or rideshare.
