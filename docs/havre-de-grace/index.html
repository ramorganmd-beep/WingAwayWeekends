Havre de Grace

A Chesapeake waterfront town with a seaplane base.

Havre de Grace sits where the Susquehanna River meets the Chesapeake Bay, with a public-use seaplane base off its waterfront. Walk the promenade to the 1827 Concord Point Lighthouse, see the carved birds at the Decoy Museum, stay in a Victorian inn and eat crabs on the water.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Harford County or on the bay.

0W3, Harford County Airport, is a non-towered field with a 3,350-foot runway about 15 minutes from town. Floatplanes and amphibians can use M06, the Havre de Grace seaplane base.

The FBO

Harford Air Services

Harford County Airport, Churchville, MD

410-836-2828

Short runway: check performance. Aberdeen Proving Ground restricted areas lie just south; brief carefully. No live fuel price is shown.

Chesapeake weather: summer haze and afternoon thunderstorms, gusty bay winds, and busy airspace near Baltimore, Aberdeen and Philadelphia. About 380 nm from Myrtle Beach.

Seaplane access

Havre de Grace (M06), public-use seaplane base

Havre de Grace, MD · manager 410-258-9648

410-258-9648

Water lanes: E/W and N/S, each 8,000 × 200 ft.

A bridge over 100 feet tall stands about 3,500 feet north of the N/S lane; birds on and near the base; coordinate use of the waterway with the appropriate agencies. (FAA chart supplement remarks.)

Water landing rules, conditions and docking change; call the base and check the chart supplement before you go.
