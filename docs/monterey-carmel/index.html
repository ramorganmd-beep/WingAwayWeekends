Monterey & Carmel

Cannery Row, cypress and storybook Carmel.

Monterey grew up around sardine canneries made famous by John Steinbeck, and Cannery Row now holds the Monterey Bay Aquarium. Next door, Carmel-by-the-Sea is a village of fairy-tale cottages, galleries and a white-sand beach, and 17-Mile Drive winds past Pebble Beach and the Lone Cypress. Land at Monterey Regional, stay in Carmel, walk Point Lobos and drive south toward Big Sur.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Monterey Regional.

KMRY, Monterey Regional, is a towered field at 257 feet with a 7,175-foot main runway between Monterey and Carmel, about 10 minutes from either town.

The FBO

Monterey Jet Center

Monterey Regional Airport, Monterey, CA

831-373-0190

Del Monte Aviation (831-648-7000) is the other FBO. Summer fog often means an instrument approach; terrain rises east of the field. No live fuel price is shown.

Central California coast: marine layer and fog much of the summer, often burning off by late morning and returning in the evening; clear, calm fall days; winter storms and gusty winds over the coastal ranges. About 2100 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Car or rideshare.

Carmel village and Cannery Row are walkable; a car is needed for 17-Mile Drive and Point Lobos.
