Martha’s Vineyard

Captains’ houses, gingerbread cottages and island sunsets.

Seven miles off Cape Cod, Martha’s Vineyard is a big, varied island of harbor towns, farms and beaches. Stroll Edgartown’s white captains’ houses, ride the oldest carousel in the country in Oak Bluffs, see the clay cliffs at Aquinnah and eat lobster on the dock at Menemsha as the sun goes down.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in the middle of the island.

KMVY, Martha’s Vineyard Airport, is a towered airport in the middle of the island, about 15 minutes from Edgartown, Oak Bluffs and Vineyard Haven.

The FBO

Martha’s Vineyard Airport FBO

71 Airport Road, Vineyard Haven, MA 02568

508-693-7022

Summer is very busy; ask the FBO about parking availability, fees and hours well ahead. No live fuel price is shown.

It is a short hop over water from Cape Cod, and fog can close the island for hours, especially in late spring and early summer. Plan a mainland alternate, carry safety gear and expect a fuel stop on the way.

Getting around

Leave the car behind.

Island buses, taxis and bike paths link the towns. Rent bikes in Edgartown or Oak Bluffs.

White captains’ houses, the harbor and Edgartown Lighthouse.
