Pilot tradition · South Carolina

The SC Breakfast Club

Since 1938, South Carolina pilots have met for breakfast every other Sunday, each time at a different small airport around the state. The breakfast is simple and inexpensive, served around 9 a.m., and everyone is welcome, whether you fly in or drive in.

The host airport changes each time, so check the club’s schedule for the next date and location.
