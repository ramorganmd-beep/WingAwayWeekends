Deep Creek Lake

A mountain lake in western Maryland, with an airport next door.

Deep Creek Lake is Maryland’s largest lake, high in the Allegheny Mountains and cool even in summer. Land at an airport right by the lake, stay at the adults-only Lake Pointe Inn, hike to Muddy Creek Falls in Swallow Falls State Park, ride the mountain coaster at Wisp and spend an afternoon on a pontoon.

Land beside the lake.
