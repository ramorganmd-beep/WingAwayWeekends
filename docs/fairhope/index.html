Fairhope & Mobile Bay

A flower-filled town on the bluffs of Mobile Bay.

Fairhope sits on a bluff above Mobile Bay, with flower baskets on every corner, a walkable downtown and a long municipal pier made for sunsets. Stay at the Grand Hotel at Point Clear, a bayfront resort since 1847, wander downtown shops and galleries, see Bellingrath Gardens and tour the battleship USS Alabama and its aircraft pavilion.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on the Eastern Shore.

KCQF, H.L. “Sonny” Callahan Airport, is a non-towered field on the east side of Fairhope, about 10 minutes from downtown.
