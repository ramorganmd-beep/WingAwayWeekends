Lake Placid

An Olympic village on a mountain lake.

High in the Adirondacks, Lake Placid hosted the Winter Olympics twice, in 1932 and 1980. Land just outside the village, walk around Mirror Lake, see the Olympic ski jumps and the rink of the “Miracle on Ice,” drive to the top of Whiteface Mountain and warm up by the fire at a lakeside lodge.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land a mile from the village.

KLKP, Lake Placid Airport, is a small mountain field about a mile south of the village.

The FBO

Adirondack Flying Service

Lake Placid Airport, 27 Airport Lane, Lake Placid, NY 12946

518-523-2473

A small field with limited services. Call ahead about fuel, tie-downs, hours and a taxi or rental car. No live fuel price is shown.

Mountain flying: high peaks surround the field, with turbulence, valley fog and fast-changing weather, and winter conditions from November to April. Check runway length and performance. About 680 nm from Myrtle Beach; plan a fuel stop.

Getting around

Walk the village.

Main Street, Mirror Lake and the Olympic Center are walkable. A car helps for Whiteface, the ski jumps and trailheads.

04 / Things to do

Olympics, lakes and peaks.

Mix one big view with one short hike.

01

The Olympic Center

Walk onto the rink of the 1980 “Miracle on Ice” and visit the Lake Placid Olympic Museum.

02

Olympic Jumping Complex

Ride the elevator to the top of the 1980 ski jumps for views over the High Peaks.

03

Whiteface Veterans Memorial Highway

Drive to near the summit of Whiteface Mountain for a 360-degree view. Seasonal.

04

Mirror Lake

An easy 2.7-mile walk around the lake from Main Street, or a canoe rental.

05

High Falls Gorge

Waterfalls and walkways through a granite gorge, 15 minutes away.
