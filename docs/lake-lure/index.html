Lake Lure

A mountain lake, back after the storm.

Lake Lure fills a steep, forested gorge below Chimney Rock, the setting for Dirty Dancing and The Last of the Mohicans. Hurricane Helene hit the gorge hard in 2024; the lake reopened in May 2026 and Chimney Rock State Park in 2025. Visiting now is a lovely weekend and real support for a community still rebuilding.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in the foothills, drive up the gorge.

KFQD, Rutherford County Airport (Marchman Field), is about 25–30 minutes from Lake Lure by car.

The FBO

Rutherford County Airport

Marchman Field · Full-service fuel

828-287-0800

The county lists full-service fuel and a courtesy car during regular hours (about 9–6 daily); directories also list 828-755-7261 for the FBO. The courtesy car suits a short trip; for a two-night stay, arrange a rental car ahead. Ask about parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

The Blue Ridge escarpment rises sharply to the west. Review terrain, the sectional and current NOTAMs, and watch for mountain weather and low clouds in the gorge.

Getting around

You’ll want a car.

The airport, the lake and Chimney Rock are spread along US 64 and 74A. Some roads and businesses in the gorge are still being repaired; check current conditions before you drive.

Choose your view of the lake.

Historic · 1927

Lake Lure Inn & Spa

The grand old hotel of the gorge, across the road from the lake. New owners plan renovations through 2026–27 while it stays open; confirm which rooms and services are available.

04 / Things to do

Up the rock, out on the water.

Check what is open before you go; recovery is still under way in places.

01

Chimney Rock State Park

Climb to the top of Chimney Rock for the view straight down Hickory Nut Gorge to the lake, and walk to Hickory Nut Falls, seen in The Last of the Mohicans. Some trails may still be closed; check before you go.

Park information
02

A boat on Lake Lure

A lake tour or a rented boat is the best way to see the gorge from the water. The lake reopened in 2026 with some temporary rules, including no-wake areas; ask what is running.

03

Lake Lure Beach

The town beach reopened for Memorial Day 2026. Pack a picnic and spend an afternoon by the water in summer.

04

Rumbling Bald and Bat Cave hikes

Granite cliffs and boulder fields on the lake’s north shore, popular with hikers and climbers.
