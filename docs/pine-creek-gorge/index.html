Pine Creek Gorge & Wellsboro

The Pennsylvania Grand Canyon and a gas-lit Main Street.

Wellsboro is a storybook town with gas lamps along a boulevard Main Street, a short drive from the Pine Creek Gorge, the Pennsylvania Grand Canyon. Look out from the rims at Leonard Harrison and Colton Point, bike or walk the Pine Creek Rail Trail along the canyon floor, and stay in a historic hotel on Main Street.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Wellsboro.

N38, Grand Canyon State Airport, is a non-towered field about 5 minutes from downtown. The main runway is 3,597 by 60 feet.

The FBO

Grand Canyon Airport Authority

Grand Canyon State Airport, Wellsboro, PA

570-724-3746

Short, narrow runway in hilly terrain: check performance, winds and density altitude. Confirm fuel, fees and a ride to town. No live fuel price is shown.

Appalachian weather: valley fog in the mornings, ridge-top turbulence, summer thunderstorms, and low ceilings and icing in winter. About 490 nm from Myrtle Beach. A long nonstop leg or a fuel stop.

Getting around

Car or taxi.

Main Street is walkable; you need a car for the rim parks and the trail.
