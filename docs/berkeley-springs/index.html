Berkeley Springs

America’s first spa, in a little mountain town.

Berkeley Springs has drawn visitors to its warm mineral springs since George Washington’s day. Soak in the historic Roman bath house at the state park, stay at the Country Inn or a cabin at Coolfont, browse galleries in the old Ice House, take in the view from Panorama Overlook and hike at Cacapon Resort State Park.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Martinsburg.

KMRB, Eastern West Virginia Regional Airport, is a towered field about 35 minutes from Berkeley Springs.
