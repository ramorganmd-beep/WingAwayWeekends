Portsmouth

A colonial harbor town with a great food scene.

Founded in 1623, Portsmouth is one of America’s oldest towns, with brick streets running down to a working harbor. Walk the historic houses of Strawbery Banke, eat oysters and tapas downtown, cruise out to the Isles of Shoals and stay at a grand 1874 hotel by the sea.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at a former Air Force base.

KPSM, Portsmouth International at Pease, is a towered former Air Force base with a long runway, about 10 minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Port City Air

104 Grafton Drive, Portsmouth, NH 03801

603-430-1111FBO details

Port City Air is open 24 hours. Ask about rental cars, parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Boston’s Class B airspace lies to the south. Coastal fog is common. About 680 nm from Myrtle Beach, so plan a fuel stop. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

Walk downtown.

Market Square, the harbor, Strawbery Banke and Prescott Park are all walkable. Use a car for New Castle and the coast.
