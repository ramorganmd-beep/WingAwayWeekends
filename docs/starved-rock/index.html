Starved Rock

Canyons, waterfalls and a historic lodge on the Illinois River.

Starved Rock State Park is a surprise in the Illinois prairie: 18 sandstone canyons with seasonal waterfalls along the Illinois River. Stay at the 1930s log lodge built by the CCC, hike to the falls in St. Louis and French canyons, explore quieter Matthiessen State Park and taste wine in Utica.

Land at Peru.

Great Lakes weather: lake-effect clouds and snow from late fall through winter, summer thunderstorms and fog over cold water in spring. About 670 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.
