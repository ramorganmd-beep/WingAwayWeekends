Santa Barbara

Red-tile roofs between the mountains and the sea.

Santa Barbara rebuilt itself in Spanish-colonial style after a 1925 earthquake, and the whitewashed walls and red-tile roofs between the Santa Ynez Mountains and the Pacific earned it the name “American Riviera.” Land at Santa Barbara Municipal near the beach, climb the courthouse clock tower for the view, visit the Old Mission, sip on the Urban Wine Trail in the Funk Zone, and drive over the mountains to Santa Ynez Valley wine country.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land by the beach.

KSBA, Santa Barbara Municipal, is a towered field at 14 feet with a 6,052-foot main runway near the beach in Goleta, about 15 minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Signature Aviation Santa Barbara

Santa Barbara Municipal Airport, Santa Barbara, CA

805-967-5608

Marine layer often keeps the field IFR in the morning; plan an afternoon arrival or be ready for an approach. Mountains rise just north. No live fuel price is shown.

Southern California coast: marine layer and low stratus most mornings from May to July (“May Gray” and “June Gloom”), usually burning off by midday; Santa Ana winds and turbulence in fall and winter; busy, complex airspace. About 2020 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Car or rideshare.

Downtown, State Street and the waterfront are walkable; a car is needed for wine country.
