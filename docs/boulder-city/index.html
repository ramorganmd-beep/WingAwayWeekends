Boulder City & Hoover Dam

The town that built Hoover Dam.

Boulder City was built in the 1930s to house the workers who built Hoover Dam, and it is still a quiet, walkable town with no casinos, just 25 minutes from Las Vegas. Land at Boulder City Municipal, the base for many Grand Canyon air tours, stay at the historic Boulder Dam Hotel, tour Hoover Dam, walk the bypass bridge for the view, and spend an afternoon on Lake Mead.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Boulder City Municipal.

KBVU, Boulder City Municipal, is a non-towered field at 2,203 feet with a 5,103-foot main runway, about five minutes from the historic downtown and 15 from Hoover Dam.

The FBO

Boulder City Aviation Services

Boulder City Municipal Airport, Boulder City, NV

702-638-3205

Busy with Grand Canyon tour helicopters and airplanes; listen carefully on CTAF. Close to the Las Vegas Class B and Hoover Dam restricted airspace. Summer density altitude is high. No live fuel price is shown.

Desert Southwest flying: summer temperatures above 110°F with very high density altitude, strong afternoon winds and turbulence, monsoon storms from July to September, and long distances between airports. About 1770 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.
