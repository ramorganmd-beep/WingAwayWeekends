Cape May

Victorian porches, the beach and a hangar full of history.

At the southern tip of New Jersey, Cape May is a seaside town of painted Victorian houses, wide beaches and long porches. Land beside a World War II hangar that is now an aviation museum, walk the historic district, watch the sunset at Cape May Point, and finish with dinner at a grand old hotel.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at a Navy field, minutes from the beach.

KWWD, Cape May County Airport, was Naval Air Station Wildwood in World War II. It sits about 15 minutes north of Cape May’s historic district.

The FBO

FlightLevel lists hours of 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. daily; after-hours fuel or service needs at least an hour’s notice and costs extra. Ask about rental cars, two-night parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Atlantic City’s Class C airspace lies to the north, and military warning areas lie offshore. Coastal fog and sea breezes are common. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

Walk once you arrive.

Cape May’s historic district, beach and restaurants are walkable, and many inns lend bikes. Use a rental car or taxi to get from the airport and out to Cape May Point.

04 / Things to do

Victorians, the lighthouse and a Navy hangar.

Choose one outing a day and leave time for the beach.

01

Naval Air Station Wildwood Aviation Museum

On the airport in Hangar No. 1, a restored World War II wooden hangar with aircraft, engines and exhibits on the dive-bomber squadrons that trained here. Listed as open daily from March through December; walk over from the FBO.

Museum information
02

The Victorian historic district

Hundreds of restored Victorian houses on shaded streets. Take a trolley or walking tour, and visit the Emlen Physick Estate.

Tours and tickets
03

Cape May Point & the lighthouse

Climb the 1859 Cape May Lighthouse, walk the state park, then watch the sunset at Sunset Beach, where the wreck of a concrete ship sits just offshore.

04

Wineries

Cape May has a small, growing wine country a short drive from town, with tastings, vineyard views and summer music.

05

Washington Street Mall & the beach

Three pedestrian blocks of shops and cafés, a short walk from the beach promenade.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

Lodging first, then dinners and a car.

Summer

July and August are busy and many inns require multi-night stays. Spring and fall are easier.

Coastal weather

Sea fog and gusty sea breezes are common. Keep a backup day.

Seasonal places

Some inns and restaurants close in winter. Check before an off-season trip.
