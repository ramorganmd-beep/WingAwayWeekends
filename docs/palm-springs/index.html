Palm Springs

Mid-century cool in the desert.

Palm Springs became Hollywood’s desert playground in the 1950s, and its low, glass-walled mid-century houses and hotels are still the star. Land at Palm Springs International, a few minutes from downtown, see the warbirds at the Palm Springs Air Museum on the field, ride the tram 8,500 feet up Mount San Jacinto, lie by a pool at a design hotel, and drive to Joshua Tree for sunset.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land minutes from downtown.

KPSP, Palm Springs International, is a towered field at 477 feet with a 10,000-foot main runway about five minutes from downtown. The Palm Springs Air Museum is on the airport.

The FBO

Signature Aviation Palm Springs

Palm Springs International Airport, Palm Springs, CA

760-327-1201

Mountains rise sharply west of the field; strong winds through the pass. Summer afternoons are very hot. No live fuel price is shown.

Desert flying: very hot summer afternoons with high density altitude, strong gusty winds through the San Gorgonio Pass, and smoke or dust some days. Best from October to May. About 1860 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Car or rideshare.

Downtown is compact; a car is needed for the tram, Joshua Tree and the other desert towns.
