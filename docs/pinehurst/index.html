Pinehurst

A short flight. A century of golf.

Longleaf pines, sandy fairways and a village built for strolling. Pinehurst is the “Home of American Golf,” but a weekend here works just as well for the partner who would rather be at the spa, on a carriage ride or browsing downtown Southern Pines.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land, then settle in.

KSOP, Moore County Airport, serves Pinehurst and Southern Pines. It is a relaxed, uncontrolled field a short drive from the village.

One FBO

Moore County Airport FBO

7825 Aviation Drive, Carthage, NC 28327

910-692-3212

The airport’s own FBO lists transient tiedowns and hangars, and crew or courtesy cars. Call ahead about two-night parking, hangar space if you want it, fees and after-hours arrangements. Directory fuel prices conflict, so no price is shown. CTAF/UNICOM 123.05.

Big golf weekends and championships bring a lot of traffic. Arrange parking early if your dates coincide with an event.

Getting around

Short hops, few rideshares.

The village and downtown Southern Pines are each a short drive from the airport, but rideshare cars can be scarce in a small town. Arrange a rental car, a local taxi or a resort transfer before you fly; ask your hotel what it offers.

Once in the village, everything is walkable.

02 / Where to stay

Choose your kind of weekend.

Grand resort hotel · The flagship

The Carolina

Pinehurst Resort’s landmark hotel, with wide verandas, formal dining and the spa next door. The best choice if golf on No. 2 is part of the plan. Ask what golf and spa access your package includes.

04 / Golf & things to do

Play a legend. Or don’t.

Golf is the headline, but the best Pinehurst weekends leave room for slower things too.

01

Pinehurst No. 2

Donald Ross’s masterpiece and a regular U.S. Open venue. Tee times are essentially reserved for resort guests, so book it with your stay. Expect a premium green fee; ask about caddies, who make the round.

Hole-by-hole tour of No. 2
02

The Cradle & Thistle Dhu

The Cradle is a fun nine-hole short course, and Thistle Dhu is a large putting course: both perfect for a partner who does not play 18 holes. Book The Cradle through the resort’s Starting Tower (910-235-8960); confirm how far ahead you can book.

The Cradle details
03

Village of Pinehurst

A planned New England-style village from the 1890s, with tree-lined lanes, historic cottages and shops. Walk it in the morning, or ask the resort about a carriage ride.

04

Downtown Southern Pines

Five minutes away: a walkable main street with independent shops, galleries and restaurants along the railroad. The Reservoir Park greenway offers an easy 2-mile loop walk.

05

Mid Pines & Pine Needles

Two classic Donald Ross courses in Southern Pines that welcome outside play. A good alternative if No. 2 is booked or out of budget.

Optional / Spa & relaxation

The Spa at Pinehurst · Time for two

Pinehurst Resort’s full-service spa, next to The Carolina, with couples massages and relaxation areas. A natural plan for the non-golfer while the other plays No. 2, or a shared treatment after golf.

Booking: Ask about couples treatments, spa access for non-resort guests, current pricing, gratuity and the cancellation policy. Weekend appointments fill early. No prices are shown here because they change, and no appointment has been booked.

Optional Saturday morning during the golf round, or Sunday before flying home.

08 / Sports & what’s happening

Sometimes the event is the reason to fly.

This prototype links to current calendars. It does not yet match events to your travel dates.

Championship golf

U.S. Open at Pinehurst

Pinehurst is a USGA “anchor site,” hosting the U.S. Open regularly, with the next scheduled for 2029. Championship weeks are a spectacle, but lodging and airport parking book up years ahead.

Peak seasons

Spring & fall golf

Spring and fall bring the best golf weather and the busiest weekends. Winter is quieter, with lower rates and mild days.

Resort calendar

Pinehurst events

The resort runs regular events, such as evening glow golf at The Cradle and seasonal celebrations. Check what is on for your dates.

09 / Suggested itinerary

Friday in. Sunday home.

A sample Friday–Sunday weekend: one round for the golfer, spa time for two, a village evening and a Southern Pines dinner. No bookings or availability confirmed.

Day trip version

Just for the day

  1. Arrive

    Land at KSOP mid-morning and head to the village.

  2. Midday

    Nine holes at The Cradle or a putting match at Thistle Dhu, then lunch at Pinehurst Brewing Co.

  3. Afternoon

    A drink at The Deuce overlooking No. 2’s 18th green, then fly home.

FRI

Arrive & unwind

  1. Late-morning arrival

    Land at KSOP, subject to the actual flight plan, and head to the resort. Ask about luggage storage if your room is not ready.

  2. Afternoon

    Lunch at Pinehurst Brewing Co., then a walk through the village, or nine holes together at The Cradle.

  3. Evening

    Dinner at Villaggio, then a nightcap on a hotel veranda.

SAT

Golf & spa

  1. Morning

    The golfer plays No. 2 (or Mid Pines or Pine Needles); the other books The Spa at Pinehurst. Or share a couples massage instead.

  2. Afternoon

    Meet for a drink at The Deuce overlooking the 18th green, then browse downtown Southern Pines.

  3. Evening

    Dinner at Elliotts on Linden.

SUN

An easy departure

  1. Morning

    Breakfast, a putting contest at Thistle Dhu and a last walk through the village.

  2. Before leaving

    Check out and return to the FBO with ample time for aircraft preparation.

  3. Fly home

    Let weather and your flight plan set the departure; there is no need to rush.

Longer stay · Add a day

With an extra day

  1. Morning

    A second round at Mid Pines or Pine Needles, or a long spa morning.

  2. Afternoon

    Browse downtown Southern Pines and walk the Reservoir Park loop.

  3. Evening

    A brewery stop in Southern Pines or Aberdeen, then a quiet dinner in the village.

10 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

Resort stay and No. 2 tee time together, then ground transport, dinners and the spa.

Allow for the season

Summers are hot and humid, with afternoon storms. Spring and fall are peak golf seasons; book early.

Dress codes

Resort dining rooms and golf courses have dress codes. Check them before you pack a light airplane.

Dates come next
