Payson & the Mogollon Rim

Pines, the Rim and a natural bridge.

Payson sits at 5,000 feet in the world’s largest ponderosa pine forest, just below the towering cliffs of the Mogollon Rim, and Phoenicians have escaped the heat here for a century. Land at Payson Airport and have lunch at the café on the field, stay at the Majestic Mountain Inn, walk beneath Tonto Natural Bridge, visit the Rim Country Museum and Zane Grey’s cabin, and drive up onto the Rim for the views.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A pine-country airport.

KPAN, Payson Airport, is a non-towered town airport at 5,160 feet with a 5,504-foot runway, about five minutes from town.

The FBO

MPG East

Payson Airport, Payson, AZ

928-970-0887

Departure noise-abatement procedure: turn north 30° for 2 nm; no arrivals or departures below 1,000 feet over town. High density altitude in summer. Crosswinds Restaurant (928-474-1613) is on the field. No live fuel price is shown.

Mountain flying: high density altitude on summer afternoons, strong winds and turbulence over the ridges, afternoon thunderstorms in summer, and winter snow and icing. About 1610 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.
