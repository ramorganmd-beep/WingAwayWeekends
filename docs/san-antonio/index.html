San Antonio

Missions, the River Walk and a hotel in an old brewery.

San Antonio is one of America’s oldest cities, founded around a chain of 18th-century Spanish missions. Land at Stinson, one of the oldest general aviation airports in the country, a few minutes from the missions. Walk the River Walk, visit the Alamo, stay in a 19th-century brewhouse at the Pearl and finish with Tex-Mex at Market Square.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at a historic airfield near the missions.

KSSF, Stinson Municipal, dates to 1915 and sits on the south side of the city, about 15 minutes from downtown and minutes from Mission San José.

The FBO

GateOne Stinson (Imperial Aviation)

8535 Mission Road, Suite 108, San Antonio, TX 78214

210-922-1203

Listed hours are about 7:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. weekdays and 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. weekends. Ask about rental cars, parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

San Antonio International’s Class C airspace and nearby military bases are busy. About 1,030 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan one or two fuel stops and three or four nights. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

Rideshare, then walk.

Downtown, the River Walk and the Pearl are linked by the river path. A car helps for the missions.
