Shawnee Hills Wine Trail

Wineries, sandstone bluffs and quiet forest in southern Illinois.

The Shawnee Hills rise out of southern Illinois with forested ridges, sandstone bluffs and a dozen wineries strung along a scenic loop. Taste at Blue Sky and StarView, hike to the rock formations at Garden of the Gods and Giant City, and stay at a historic schoolhouse inn or a lodge in the woods.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Marion.

KMWA, Veterans Airport of Southern Illinois, is a towered field about 30 minutes from the wine trail and Giant City.
