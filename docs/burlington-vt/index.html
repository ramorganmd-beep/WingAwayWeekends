Burlington

Lake Champlain sunsets and a lively small city.

Burlington is a walkable small city on the shore of Lake Champlain, with the Adirondacks across the water. Stroll the Church Street Marketplace, bike the lakefront path, cruise the lake at sunset, visit Shelburne Farms and the Shelburne Museum and eat at Hen of the Wood.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Burlington.

KBTV, Burlington International, is a towered airport about 10 minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Heritage Aviation

Burlington International Airport, South Burlington, VT

802-863-3626

Confirm FBO hours, fees and ground transportation. No live fuel price is shown.

Mountain and lake weather: watch ceilings, icing in the cold months and afternoon storms in summer. About 700 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.
