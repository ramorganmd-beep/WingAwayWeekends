Marquette & Pictured Rocks

Lake Superior and painted cliffs.

Marquette is the largest town on Michigan’s Upper Peninsula, a former iron-ore port on Lake Superior with a lively downtown, a towering ore dock and beaches you can walk to. Land at Sawyer International, stay at the 1930 Landmark Inn above the harbor, walk Presque Isle Park, and drive an hour east to Munising for a boat cruise under the colored cliffs of Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A long runway near Marquette.

KSAW, Sawyer International, is a towered former Air Force base at 1,221 feet with a 9,072-foot runway, about 25 minutes south of Marquette and an hour from Munising.

The FBO

Kubick Aviation Services

Sawyer International Airport, Gwinn, MI

906-346-6440

A large former military field with airline service; confirm FBO hours and arrange a rental car ahead. Lake Superior weather changes quickly; check the forecast for fog and snow showers. No live fuel price is shown.

Great Lakes flying: lake-effect snow and low ceilings from November to March, fog and low clouds off the lakes, summer thunderstorms, and long stretches over cold water and forest with few airports. About 850 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Rental car.

You need a car: Marquette is 25 minutes away and Munising about an hour.
