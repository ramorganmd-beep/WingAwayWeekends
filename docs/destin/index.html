Destin

Emerald water, a working harbor and an airport minutes from the beach.

Destin Executive sits right in town, a few minutes from the beach and the harbor. Stay at a beach resort, eat fresh Gulf seafood along the harbor, take a boat out to Crab Island, walk the Village of Baytowne Wharf at Sandestin and, for pilots, see the aircraft at the Air Force Armament Museum near Eglin.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land minutes from the harbor.

KDTS, Destin Executive, has a 5,001 ft runway (14/32) and sits in town between the harbor and the beach. Atlantic Aviation is the FBO. Eglin Air Force Base is next door.

Busy military airspace (Eglin) surrounds the field: check the chart supplement, restricted areas and NOTAMs before you go. Summer afternoons bring thunderstorms.

Getting around

Rental car or Uber.

The beach and the harbor are a few minutes from the airport by Uber. Rent a car for Sandestin, Baytowne Wharf and the Armament Museum.
