Louisville

Churchill Downs, Whiskey Row and the Hot Brown.

Louisville is home to the Kentucky Derby and the heart of bourbon country. Tour Churchill Downs at the Kentucky Derby Museum, sample on Whiskey Row, eat a Hot Brown where it was invented at the Brown Hotel, swing a bat at the Louisville Slugger Museum, visit the Muhammad Ali Center and walk the Big Four Bridge across the Ohio.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Bowman Field.

KLOU, Bowman Field, is a towered GA airport, one of the oldest in the country, about 15 minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Louisville Executive Aviation

Bowman Field, Louisville, KY

502-292-4800

Confirm FBO hours, fees and cars. No live fuel price is shown.

Bowman sits under the Louisville Class C. Derby week brings very heavy traffic and special procedures. About 425 nm from Myrtle Beach.
