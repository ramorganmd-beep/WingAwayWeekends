Yadkin Valley

North Carolina wine country, one runway away.

Elkin sits in the middle of the Yadkin Valley, the state’s first wine region, with dozens of wineries scattered across the foothills below the Blue Ridge. Land a few minutes from town, taste at estate vineyards with mountain views, and end the day with dinner among the vines.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land just outside town.

KZEF, Elkin Municipal, is a non-towered field beside the Yadkin River, a few minutes from downtown Elkin.

The FBO

Yadkin Valley Aviation

1598 C.C. Camp Road, Elkin, NC 28621

336-366-3433

Yadkin Valley Aviation lists self-serve 100LL and Jet A and a courtesy car, with staff on site during the day (about 8–5). Pilots report a small tie-down fee and an after-hours call-out fee. A rental car can be arranged with notice; call ahead. CTAF 123.05. No live fuel price is shown.

Pilot Mountain and the Blue Ridge rise to the north and west. Review terrain, the sectional and current NOTAMs, and watch for afternoon mountain weather in summer.

Getting around

Let someone else drive the wine roads.

Wineries are spread out, 10–30 minutes apart. Use a car for town and dinner, and book the Elkin Vine Line wine shuttle (Friday–Sunday from the downtown visitor center) or a private driver for the tasting day.

Pilot note / Wine & flying

Taste on the non-flying day.

FAA rules (14 CFR 91.17) require at least 8 hours from bottle to throttle, a blood alcohol below 0.04% and no lingering effects. Many pilots use 12–24 hours as a personal minimum, since a hangover can impair you long after the legal limit is met. Plan the tasting day for the middle of the weekend, keep the evening before departure dry, and let a driver or shuttle handle the roads. If the pilot wants a full tasting day, add a third night. The Elkin Vine Line shuttle runs Friday–Sunday; check its current schedule and book seats.

Tasting hours and reservation rules change by season; many wineries require reservations for groups or on weekends. Confirm before you go.

05 / Things to do

Between the tastings.

Small-town evenings and big foothills views.

01

Downtown Elkin

A walkable main street with shops, cafés and the restored Reeves Theater, which hosts live music most weekends.

02

Pilot Mountain State Park

The famous quartzite knob is a landmark from the air; from the ground, short trails lead to wide overlooks. About 30 minutes east.

03

Stone Mountain State Park

A granite dome, waterfalls and hiking trails about 30 minutes northwest. A good Sunday-morning walk before the flight home.

04

Elkin & Alleghany Rail Trail

An easy walk or bike ride from town along an old rail bed, part of North Carolina’s Mountains-to-Sea Trail route.

05

Mount Airy

Andy Griffith’s hometown, the model for Mayberry, about 30 minutes north. Nostalgic and fun for an hour or two.

07 / Suggested itinerary

Friday in. Sunday home.

A sample Friday–Sunday weekend with the tasting day in the middle. No bookings or availability confirmed.

Day trip version

Just for the day

  1. Arrive

    Land at KZEF mid-morning and pick up the courtesy car or a rental.

  2. Midday

    Lunch and a tasting for the non-pilot at Elkin Creek Vineyard.

  3. Afternoon

    A glass on the terrace at Raffaldini for the non-pilot, then back to the FBO before it closes.

FRI

Arrive & unwind

  1. Late-morning arrival

    Land at KZEF, subject to weather and your actual flight plan, and pick up the car.

  2. Afternoon

    Lunch at Elkin Creek Vineyard and check in. The non-pilot can enjoy a glass.

  3. Evening

    Dinner at Wisdom Table in downtown Elkin.

SAT

Wine country day

  1. Late morning

    Ride the Vine Line or a private driver to Raffaldini for a tasting on the terrace.

  2. Afternoon

    Jones von Drehle, then one more stop. Water and lunch between tastings.

  3. Evening

    Dinner at JOLO. Keep the night early if you fly tomorrow.

SUN

An easy departure

  1. Morning

    Breakfast in Elkin and a walk at Stone Mountain or Pilot Mountain.

  2. Before leaving

    Return the car and allow time at the FBO, which keeps daytime hours.

  3. Fly home

    Only when the pilot is well clear of the 8-hour rule and feels fully fit to fly.

Longer stay · Add a day

With an extra day

  1. Morning

    Hike to the overlooks at Pilot Mountain or Stone Mountain State Park.

  2. Afternoon

    Downtown Elkin, or a side trip to Mount Airy.

  3. Evening

    Live music at the Reeves Theater.
