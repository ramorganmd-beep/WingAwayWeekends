Newport

Mansions, sailboats and the Cliff Walk.

Newport is where Gilded Age families built their summer “cottages” above the Atlantic, and where America’s Cup sailors raced for more than 50 years. Walk the Cliff Walk past the mansions, tour The Breakers, sail on the harbor at sunset, and end with dinner at an oceanfront inn.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land five minutes from town.

KUUU, Newport State Airport, is a small, non-towered field in Middletown, about 10 minutes from downtown Newport.

The FBO

FlightLevel Aviation · Newport

211 Airport Access Road, Middletown, RI 02842

401-846-9400FBO details

FlightLevel lists hours of 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. and rental cars through Enterprise with prior arrangement. Ask about weekend tie-downs and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

At about 600 nm from Myrtle Beach, most piston aircraft will need a fuel stop. Coastal fog is common, and Providence’s Class C airspace lies to the north. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

Walk once you arrive.

Downtown, the harbor and Bellevue Avenue are walkable or a short ride apart. A car helps for Ocean Drive and Castle Hill.

04 / Things to do

Mansions, cliffs and the sea.

Choose one big outing a day and leave time for the water.

01

The Newport Mansions

Tour The Breakers, the Vanderbilts’ 70-room summer “cottage,” and one or two others, such as Marble House or The Elms.

Mansion tickets
02

The Cliff Walk

A 3.5-mile path along the cliffs between the mansions and the ocean. The northern half from Memorial Boulevard is the easiest walk.

03

Ocean Drive

A 10-mile coastal drive past rocky coves, Brenton Point and grand houses. Best in the late afternoon.

04

A sail on the harbor

Sunset sails on classic schooners and America’s Cup yachts leave from the downtown wharves in season.

05

Fort Adams & Bowen’s Wharf

A huge 19th-century fort at the harbor mouth, then shops and raw bars along the wharves.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

Lodging and a car first, then dinners, mansion tickets and a sail.

Fuel stop

Plan a fuel stop on the way for most piston aircraft.

Summer

July and August are busy and pricey. Spring and fall are easier.

Coastal weather

Fog and sea breezes are common. Keep a backup day.
