Partner with WingAway Weekends

WingAway Weekends helps private pilots and their passengers plan short trips: where to land, where to stay, where to eat and what to do once the airplane is tied down.

We cover hundreds of destinations in all 50 states, plus Fly to Lunch, a list of restaurants on or near airports. Every guide links to the official websites of the places we recommend. We don’t take payment for recommendations, and we label anything sponsored.

Our principle: powerful underneath, effortless on the surface. Behind each simple page are FAA airport data, NOAA forecasts and aircraft performance numbers; what pilots and their passengers see is a calm page with one clear next step.

Ways to work with us

Share photos and updates

Send a photo we can use with credit, or tell us what’s changed: new hours, a new restaurant, a renovation. Free, and it makes the guide better.

Welcome visiting pilots

Airport shuttles, crew cars, fly-in discounts and pilot packages are exactly what our readers look for. Tell us about yours and we’ll add it to the guide.

Sponsor a guide or the newsletter

A clearly labeled sponsor spot in a destination guide or the monthly email. Ask us for details and availability.

Who reads it

  • Private pilots planning weekend and day trips, often with a spouse, partner or family.
  • Readers who arrive by airplane, stay one to three nights and spend locally on hotels, restaurants and activities.
  • Pilots looking for airport restaurants, ground transportation and fly-in-friendly places.
