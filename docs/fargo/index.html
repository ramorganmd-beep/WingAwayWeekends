Fargo

An art hotel, a classic theater and an air museum.

Fargo’s downtown has become one of the Plains’ liveliest, with a restored art deco theater and good restaurants. Stay at the art-filled Hotel Donaldson and have dinner at its HoDo restaurant, see a film at the Fargo Theatre, visit the Plains Art Museum and spend a morning at the Fargo Air Museum.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Hector.

KFAR, Hector International, is a towered airport about 10 minutes from downtown. The Fargo Air Museum is on the field.

The FBO

Fargo Jet Center

Hector International Airport, Fargo, ND

High plains weather: strong winds, summer thunderstorms and fast-moving winter systems. Check density altitude in summer. About 1140 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.

Getting around

Walk downtown.

Downtown is walkable; a rideshare covers the airport.
