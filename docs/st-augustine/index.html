St. Augustine

The oldest city. A long weekend’s worth of stories.

Coquina walls, narrow Spanish streets, Henry Flagler’s grand hotels and sunsets over Matanzas Bay. St. Augustine is the longest flight in this collection from KMYR and one of the most rewarding.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land, then head for the old city.

KSGJ, Northeast Florida Regional Airport, is a towered field about 15–20 minutes north of the historic district.

Full service or self-serve

Atlantic Aviation

4900 US Highway 1 North, Suite 100, St. Augustine, FL 32095

904-824-1995FBO details

Atlantic Aviation is the FBO. Directory listings also show self-serve 100LL on the field. Ask Atlantic about facility fees, two-night parking, waivers with fuel and after-hours fees. Prices vary widely between listings, so call ahead; no live price is shown.

The airport authority can be reached at 904-209-0090.

Getting around

Ride in. Walk the old city.

Rideshare or a taxi to the historic district takes about 15–20 minutes. Once there, everything in this guide is walkable or a short trolley ride.

Parking downtown is tight, so a rental car is only worth it if you plan to visit the beaches.

03 / Where to eat

Two dinners. One long lunch.

The historic district is busy on weekends, so reserve your anchor dinner early.

Friday dinner · Intimate

Collage

A small, romantic restaurant known for globally inspired cooking and live piano. The classic St. Augustine date night. Reserve well ahead.

Official details for Collage
Saturday dinner · Peruvian

Llama

A tiny, much-loved Peruvian restaurant on Anastasia Island, near the lighthouse, with about ten tables and a chef from Lima. Ceviche and lomo saltado are favorites. Book well ahead; closed some weekdays.

Reservations for Llama
Lunch · Spanish-Cuban

The Columbia

A long-running Spanish and Cuban restaurant on St. George Street. A leisurely lunch between sights; try the 1905 salad.

Official details for The Columbia
04 / Things to do

Walk five centuries.

Choose a few memorable experiences; the old city is compact enough to wander between them.

01

Castillo de San Marcos

The 17th-century Spanish stone fort on the bay, a National Park Service site. Go early or late in the day to avoid the heat and crowds.

Park information
02

St. Augustine Lighthouse & Maritime Museum

Climb the striped lighthouse for the view in this guide’s photo, then explore the maritime museum. Across the bridge on Anastasia Island.

Lighthouse information
03

Flagler College & the Gilded Age

Henry Flagler’s 1888 Hotel Ponce de Leon is now Flagler College. Ask about guided historic tours of its Tiffany-glass dining hall and grand rotunda.

04

St. George Street & Lincolnville

The pedestrian heart of the old city, busy by day. For a quieter walk, wander Lincolnville’s historic streets or the bayfront at sunset.

05

Sunset on Matanzas Bay

Several operators run sunset sails and cruises from the downtown marina. Choose one water outing and book a day or two ahead.

Optional / Spa & relaxation

The spa at Casa Monica · Time for two

Casa Monica Resort & Spa has a full-service spa in the heart of the historic district, a good fit for a couples treatment after a morning of walking.

05 / Wine, spirits & cocktails

A glass, and no hurry.

Two downtown tasting stops and a cocktail lounge across the bridge.

King Street · Winery

San Sebastian Winery

A working winery at 157 King Street with tours and tastings of Florida wines, plus a rooftop bar. Check tour times and the rooftop’s hours.

Official details for San Sebastian Winery
Distillery · Tours

St. Augustine Distillery

Craft spirits made in the restored ice plant, with distillery tours and tastings. Tours fill up on weekends; book ahead.

Official details for St. Augustine Distillery
Cocktails · Anastasia Island

Odd Birds Cocktail Lounge

An inventive cocktail lounge with a speakeasy feel, just over the Bridge of Lions and a short walk from Llama. Perfect for a drink before or after Saturday dinner. For a downtown alternative, try Forgotten Tonic on Aviles Street.

Hours & details for Odd Birds Cocktail Lounge
06 / Shopping & take home

Datil heat, chocolate and the old town’s shops.

St. George Street is the pedestrian heart; Aviles Street, the oldest street in town, holds the galleries.

Historic district · Pedestrian street

St. George Street

Shops, sweets and souvenirs in the old town’s car-free main street. Go early or late to avoid the midday crowds.

Website & map for St. George Street
Historic district · Galleries

Aviles Street

Small galleries and local art on the oldest street in the city, a short walk south of the Plaza.

Local flavors to take home

St. Augustine’s own hot sauce, made with the datil pepper the city’s Minorcan families have grown for generations. Sweet heat; sold around town and online.

08 / Suggested itinerary

Friday in. Sunday home.

A sample Friday–Sunday weekend: the fort, the Flagler buildings, a sunset on the bay and two very different dinners. No bookings or availability confirmed.

Day trip version

Just for the day

  1. Arrive

    Land at KSGJ early and ride into the old city.

  2. Midday

    Castillo de San Marcos, then lunch at The Columbia on St. George Street.

  3. Afternoon

    A San Sebastian Winery tasting or a Flagler College tour, then fly home ahead of afternoon storms.

FRI

Arrive & unwind

  1. Midday arrival

    Land at KSGJ, subject to weather and your actual flight plan, and ride into the old city.

  2. Afternoon

    Lunch at The Columbia, then a slow walk down St. George Street. Check in when your room is ready.

  3. Evening

    Dinner at Collage if reserved.

SAT

Fort, hotels & bay

  1. Morning

    Castillo de San Marcos when it opens, then a Flagler College tour.

  2. Afternoon

    San Sebastian Winery tasting, or a couples treatment at Casa Monica. Late afternoon: the lighthouse climb.

  3. Evening

    Sunset on Matanzas Bay, cocktails at Odd Birds, then dinner at Llama.

SUN

An easy departure

  1. Morning

    Breakfast and a quiet walk through Lincolnville or along the bayfront.

  2. Before leaving

    Check out and allow ample time at the FBO.

  3. Fly home

    Plan around afternoon storms in warm months; a morning departure is often smoother.

09 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

Lodging first, then the Friday dinner, a Flagler College tour and one water outing.

Allow for the season

Summer heat and daily storms; holiday lights season is crowded. Spring and fall are easiest.

Wear good shoes

Old streets are uneven, and the lighthouse has a lot of steps.
