Wichita Mountains & Medicine Park

Bison, granite peaks and a cobblestone creek town.

The Wichita Mountains rise abruptly out of the southern Oklahoma prairie. Drive to the top of Mount Scott, watch bison and longhorn graze in the Wichita Mountains Wildlife Refuge, hike to granite boulders and lakes, stay in a cobblestone cabin in Medicine Park, a 1908 resort town on a creek, and eat a longhorn Meersburger at a ramshackle store in Meers.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Lawton.

KLAW, Lawton–Fort Sill Regional, is a towered airport with an 8,600-foot runway, about 20 minutes from Medicine Park.

The FBO

Plains weather: strong winds, spring and summer thunderstorms (this is tornado country), summer heat and density altitude, and occasional winter ice storms. About 970 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.
