Halter Ranch Fly-In Winery

Land in the vineyard, walk to lunch.

Halter Ranch is a 2,500-acre organic estate in the hills west of Paso Robles, and it calls itself the only fly-in winery and vineyard in the world. By prior permission, you can land on its private airstrip, walk to lunch at Restaurant Alice and the estate tasting room, tour the vineyards and fly home with wine. Wine club members can stay in the Pilot Cottages by the creek; everyone else can book an estate house or stay in Paso Robles, 11 nm east.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A private strip in the vineyards: ask first.

89CA is a private-use airstrip: you must request a fly-in date from Halter Ranch at least 3 days ahead. One asphalt runway, 4/22, 3,353 ft long and only 25 ft wide; field elevation 1,488 ft. No fuel or services are listed.

Checked Nov 2026. Crew cars and pilot perks change often: call the FBO before you go. How to have a rental car waiting at the FBO · Know something we missed? Tell us.

Request your date through the winery’s fly-in page or by phone at least 3 days ahead, and wait for their confirmation. Check that your insurance covers private airstrips.

The runway is very narrow (25 ft) in hilly terrain: plan a stable approach, check crosswinds and your landing and takeoff numbers, and avoid hot, gusty afternoons. Fuel up beforehand: Paso Robles (KPRB), 11 nm east, has fuel and a full-service FBO.

Morning fog and low clouds are common on the Central Coast, especially in late spring and summer.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Prior permission

Private-use airstrip: request a date at least 3 days ahead and wait for confirmation.

Narrow runway

25 ft wide and 3,353 ft long, at 1,488 ft elevation in the hills: check your numbers and crosswind.

Fuel

None listed on the field. KPRB, 11 nm east, has fuel and an FBO.

Pilot in command

Wine on board is cargo, not lunch: no tasting before you fly.
