Bentonville & Crystal Bridges

World-class art, forest trails and coffee on the ramp.

Bentonville, home of Walmart, has become one of the country’s surprise art and outdoor towns. Walk the forest trails to Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art, see contemporary art at the Momentary, ride some of the best mountain-bike trails in the country, and have coffee at a café on the airport named for aviation pioneer Louise Thaden.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Louise Thaden Field.

KVBT, Bentonville Municipal–Louise M. Thaden Field, is a few minutes from downtown. It is named for the Bentonville-born pilot who won the first women’s air derby and the 1936 Bendix race.

The FBO

Legends Air Center

2205 SW I Street, Bentonville, AR 72712

479-254-0817

Sources disagree on the FBO phone number, so confirm it. Ask about rental cars, bikes, parking and fees. Airship Coffee is on the field. No live fuel price is shown.

Northwest Arkansas National (KXNA) and Razorback Approach are nearby. About 770 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and consider a third night. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

Bikes do most of the work.

Paved trails link the square, Crystal Bridges and the Momentary. Rent bikes downtown, or drive the short distances.
