Memphis

Blues, soul, rock ’n’ roll and barbecue on the Mississippi.

Memphis is where the blues found a home and rock ’n’ roll was born. Watch the Peabody ducks march, tour Graceland and the tiny Sun Studio where Elvis first recorded, hear soul history at Stax, spend a sobering morning at the National Civil Rights Museum and finish with ribs and live blues on Beale Street.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Memphis.

KMEM, Memphis International, is a busy towered airport and FedEx’s home base, about 15 minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Wilson Air Center

Memphis International Airport, Memphis, TN

901-345-2992

Wilson Air Center is a well-regarded GA FBO. General DeWitt Spain Airport (M01), just north of downtown, is a quieter non-towered alternative (901-358-0028). Confirm hours, fees and cars. No live fuel price is shown.

Expect heavy cargo traffic at night and a Class B arrival. About 555 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop or a long nonstop leg.

Getting around

Rideshare downtown.

Downtown and Beale Street are walkable; use a car or rideshare for Graceland, Stax and Sun Studio.
