St. Louis

The Arch, Forest Park and dinner on The Hill.

St. Louis has some of the country’s best free attractions. Ride the tram to the top of the Gateway Arch, spend a day in Forest Park at the Art Museum, walk the Missouri Botanical Garden, play at the wild City Museum, eat toasted ravioli on The Hill and finish with frozen custard at Ted Drewes on old Route 66.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land across the river.

KCPS, St. Louis Downtown Airport, is a towered field in Cahokia, Illinois, about 15 minutes from the Arch and downtown.

The FBO

Jet Aviation

St. Louis Downtown Airport, Cahokia Heights, IL

618-646-8262

Spirit of St. Louis Airport (KSUS), west of the city, is an alternative with a Signature FBO (636-532-8882) and is closer to Forest Park and the western suburbs. Confirm hours, fees and cars. No live fuel price is shown.

Brief the St. Louis Class B. About 620 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Rent a car or rideshare.

Downtown is compact, but Forest Park, The Hill and Ted Drewes are spread out.
