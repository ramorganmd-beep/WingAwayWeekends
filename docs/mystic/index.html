Mystic

Tall ships, a drawbridge and lobster by the water.

Mystic was a 19th-century shipbuilding town, and its waterfront still feels like it. Explore tall ships at Mystic Seaport Museum, watch the drawbridge rise downtown, walk the quiet streets of Stonington Borough, and tour the world’s first nuclear-powered submarine a few minutes from the airport.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land 10 minutes from Mystic.

KGON, Groton–New London Airport, is a towered field on the water, about 10 minutes from downtown Mystic.

The FBO

Modern Aviation · Groton

193 Tower Avenue, Groton, CT 06340

860-715-8800

Directories list hours of 5 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily; one source lists the address as 175 Tower Avenue, so confirm it. Ask about rental cars, parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Coastal fog and sea breezes are common, and the Navy submarine base lies just up the Thames River. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs, and plan a fuel stop for most piston aircraft.

Getting around

A car, then your feet.

Downtown Mystic is walkable, and the Seaport is a short walk up the river. A car helps for Stonington, Noank and the submarine museum.

04 / Things to do

Ships, submarines and old villages.

One big outing a day, and time on the water.

01

Mystic Seaport Museum

A re-created 19th-century seafaring village with historic ships, including the Charles W. Morgan, the last wooden whaling ship. Allow half a day.

Museum information
02

Submarine Force Museum & USS Nautilus

Go aboard the world’s first nuclear-powered submarine in Groton, minutes from the airport. Free.

03

Mystic Aquarium

Beluga whales, seals and penguins, a short drive from downtown.

Tickets
04

Stonington Borough

A quiet 18th-century fishing village on a point, with antique shops and a lighthouse museum.

05

The drawbridge & the river

Watch the 1922 bascule bridge rise downtown, then take a river cruise or a sail.
