Walla Walla Wine Country

Wine country with tasting rooms by the runway.

Walla Walla has grown into one of the country’s great wine regions, with more than 120 wineries around a tree-lined downtown full of tasting rooms and restaurants. Land at Walla Walla Regional, where some wineries are in the old World War II buildings next to the airport, stay at the 1928 Marcus Whitman Hotel or the Eritage Resort among the vineyards, taste along Main Street, and have dinner at Brasserie Four or Saffron.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Walla Walla.

KALW, Walla Walla Regional, is a towered airport at 1,194 feet with a 6,527-foot runway, about five minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Gorge Aviation Services

Walla Walla Regional Airport, Walla Walla, WA

509-540-1081

Hot, gusty summer afternoons and winter valley fog. Several wineries are in the airport district. No live fuel price is shown.

Inland Northwest flying: hot, dry summers with afternoon winds and wildfire smoke, cold winters with fog and low ceilings in the valleys, and the Cascades to the west with icing in winter clouds. About 1940 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Car or wine driver.

Downtown is walkable; hire a driver or tour for the vineyards.
