North Conway & Jackson

A mountain valley of inns, a scenic railroad and covered bridges.

The Mount Washington Valley is a classic New England mountain getaway. Stay in Jackson, through its red covered bridge, ride the Conway Scenic Railroad through Crawford Notch, drive the Kancamagus Highway, walk to Diana’s Baths and drive the Auto Road up Mount Washington.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Fryeburg.

KIZG, Eastern Slopes Regional Airport in Fryeburg, Maine, is a non-towered field about 15 minutes from North Conway.

The FBO

Mountain and lake weather: watch ceilings, icing in the cold months and afternoon storms in summer. About 720 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.
