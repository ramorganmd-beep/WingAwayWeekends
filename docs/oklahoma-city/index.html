Oklahoma City

Cowboy heritage, a moving memorial and a skyline dinner.

Oklahoma City blends cowboy history with a lively, rebuilt downtown. Spend a quiet hour at the Oklahoma City National Memorial, see the First Americans Museum and the National Cowboy & Western Heritage Museum, ride the Bricktown water taxi, eat steak at Cattlemen’s in Stockyards City, and have dinner at Vast atop the Devon Tower.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Wiley Post.

KPWA, Wiley Post Airport, is a towered GA airport about 20 minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Atlantic Aviation

Wiley Post Airport, Oklahoma City, OK

405-787-4040

Wiley Post has several FBOs. Confirm hours, fees and cars. No live fuel price is shown.

Plains weather: strong winds, spring and summer thunderstorms (this is tornado country), summer heat and density altitude, and occasional winter ice storms. About 930 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.

Getting around

Rent a car or rideshare.

Downtown and Bricktown are walkable; the museums are a short drive.
