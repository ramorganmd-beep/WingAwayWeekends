Hershey

A grand hilltop hotel, a chocolate spa and gardens.

Milton Hershey built a town around his chocolate factory, and his 1933 hilltop hotel is still its centerpiece. Book a whipped-cocoa bath at the spa, walk the rose gardens and butterfly house at Hershey Gardens, make your own candy bar at The Hershey Story, and taste wine nearby.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Capital City.

KCXY, Capital City Airport, is a towered GA field near Harrisburg, about 20 minutes from Hershey.

The FBO

Appalachian weather: valley fog in the mornings, ridge-top turbulence, summer thunderstorms, and low ceilings and icing in winter. About 400 nm from Myrtle Beach.
