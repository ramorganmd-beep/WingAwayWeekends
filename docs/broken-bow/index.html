Broken Bow & Beavers Bend

Hot-tub cabins in the pines by a clear lake and river.

Broken Bow is Oklahoma’s favorite couples’ hideaway: thousands of cabins tucked into the pines around Hochatown, with hot tubs and fire pits. Paddle or fish the clear Mountain Fork River in Beavers Bend State Park, boat on Broken Bow Lake, taste spirits at Hochatown Distilling and share a pizza at Grateful Head.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Idabel.

4O4, McCurtain County Regional, is a non-towered field with a 5,000-foot runway, about 30 minutes from Hochatown.

The FBO

City of Idabel (FBO)

McCurtain County Regional Airport, Idabel, OK

580-286-3558

Broken Bow’s own airport (90F) has only a 3,200-by-50-foot runway; Idabel is the better choice for most. Confirm fuel and a car. No live fuel price is shown.

Plains weather: strong winds, spring and summer thunderstorms (this is tornado country), summer heat and density altitude, and occasional winter ice storms. About 790 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Rent a car.

The cabins, park and restaurants are spread out; a car is essential.
