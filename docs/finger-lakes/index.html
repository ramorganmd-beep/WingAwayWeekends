Finger Lakes

Waterfalls, wine trails and long, glacial lakes.

The Finger Lakes are long, deep glacial lakes lined with vineyards, and Ithaca sits at the foot of Cayuga Lake surrounded by gorges. Hike past the 19 waterfalls of Watkins Glen, see Taughannock Falls, taste Riesling along the Cayuga and Seneca wine trails, stay at the Inns of Aurora on the lake and browse the Ithaca Farmers Market.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Ithaca.

KITH, Ithaca Tompkins International, is a towered airport about 10 minutes from downtown Ithaca.

The FBO

Taughannock Aviation

Ithaca Tompkins International Airport, Ithaca, NY

607-257-7500

Lake-effect clouds and snow are common from late fall through winter. About 540 nm from Myrtle Beach. A long nonstop leg or a fuel stop.

Getting around

Rent a car.

The wineries, gorges and Aurora are spread around the lakes; you need a car and a designated driver.
