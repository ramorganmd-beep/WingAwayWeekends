New Haven

Pizza, Yale and world-class free museums.

New Haven is famous for its coal-fired “apizza,” and the lines at Frank Pepe and Sally’s are part of the fun. Between pizzas, see the free Yale University Art Gallery and Yale Center for British Art, the dinosaurs at the Peabody, a show at Yale Rep and the burger at Louis’ Lunch.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Tweed.

KHVN, Tweed–New Haven Airport, is a towered field about 10 minutes from downtown and Yale.

The FBO

Getting around

Walk downtown.

Yale and downtown are walkable; Wooster Street’s pizza is a short ride.
