Grand Marais & the North Shore

Lake Superior cliffs, waterfalls and a harbor arts town.

The North Shore runs along the rocky edge of Lake Superior, with waterfalls tumbling out of the hills every few miles. Base yourself in Grand Marais, an arts town around a small harbor. Walk out to Artist’s Point, ride the Lutsen gondola, see the Devil’s Kettle falls, drive the Gunflint Trail and eat smoked fish and doughnuts by the water.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Grand Marais.

KCKC, Grand Marais/Cook County Airport, is a non-towered field with a 5,000-foot runway in the hills above town, about 15 minutes from the harbor.

The FBO

Roy Aero Service

Grand Marais/Cook County Airport, Grand Marais, MN

218-387-3024

Confirm fuel, fees and a rental car or ride before you go. No live fuel price is shown.

Lake Superior weather: fog and low clouds off the cold lake, summer thunderstorms, and long, snowy winters with icing. About 1000 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.

Seaplane access

Grand Marais/Cook County (0G5), public-use seaplane base

Grand Marais, MN · 218-387-3024

218-387-3024

Water lanes: 03W/21W 3,000 × 2,000 ft; 09W/27W 15,000 × 1,500 ft.

Customs available May to October: call 218-475-2244. After hours: 218-387-2012. (FAA chart supplement remarks.)

Lake Superior is cold and can be rough; Gunflint Lodge (M56) on the Gunflint Trail also has a public seaplane base.

Water landing rules, conditions and docking change; call the base and check the chart supplement before you go.
