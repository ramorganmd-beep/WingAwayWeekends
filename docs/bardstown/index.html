Bardstown & Bourbon Country

The Bourbon Capital of the World.

Bardstown is the heart of Kentucky bourbon country, with more than a dozen distilleries within a short drive and a historic downtown of brick buildings around the courthouse square. Tour the rickhouses, taste at Heaven Hill and Willett, visit Maker’s Mark in its pretty hollow, and dine in a tavern that has served travelers since 1779.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land five minutes from the square.

KBRY, Samuels Field, is a small non-towered airport just outside Bardstown, about five minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Samuels Field Aviation

1924 Boston Road, Bardstown, KY 40004

502-348-7858

Directories list hours of 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday to Saturday, unattended on Sunday, and a courtesy car. Confirm the car, parking and fees ahead. No live fuel price is shown.

Bourbon and flying: FAA rules require at least 8 hours from “bottle to throttle” and a blood alcohol level under 0.04%, and many pilots set 12 to 24 hours as a personal minimum. Taste on the non-flying day, and hire a driver or a bourbon tour company; don’t drive between distilleries after tasting.

Getting around

Hire a driver for tasting days.

Downtown Bardstown is walkable. Local tour companies and drivers run distillery days, the safest way to taste.
