Lancaster & Amish Country

Farmland, a steam train and the oldest market in America.

Lancaster County is a patchwork of Amish farms, covered bridges and small towns, with a lively historic city at its center. Shop America’s oldest farmers market, ride a steam train through the fields, drive the back roads past horse-drawn buggies and finish with dinner in a converted factory.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land between Lancaster and Lititz.

KLNS, Lancaster Airport, is a towered field just north of the city, about 15 minutes from downtown Lancaster and 10 from Lititz.

The FBO

Alliance Aviation

500 Airport Road, Suite G, Lititz, PA 17543

717-735-9507

Directories list hours of about 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily. Ask about rental cars, two-night parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Harrisburg’s airspace lies to the west. Review the sectional, current NOTAMs and TFRs.

Getting around

You’ll want a car.

The farms, the railroad and the small towns are spread across the county. Drive slowly and give horse-drawn buggies plenty of room.

04 / Things to do

Farms, trains and small towns.

Take the back roads, and leave time for the farm stands.

01

Strasburg Rail Road

America’s oldest operating short-line railroad, with steam trains through Amish farmland. Ride in a first-class or dining car.

Train schedule
02

Railroad Museum of Pennsylvania

Across the road, a huge hall of historic locomotives and cars.

Museum information
03

Amish Farm and House

A guided tour of a working farm and house, and a respectful introduction to Amish life.

Tour information
04

Lititz

A pretty Moravian town with shops, a park and the Julius Sturgis Pretzel Bakery, the first commercial pretzel bakery in America.

Pretzel bakery
05

A back-roads drive

Covered bridges, farm stands and quilt shops around Bird-in-Hand, Intercourse and Strasburg. Many shops close on Sundays.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

A car and lodging first, then dinners and train tickets.

Market days

Central Market is open Tuesday, Friday and Saturday mornings only.

Sundays

Many Amish-run shops and farm stands close on Sundays.

Respect

Ask before photographing Amish people; many prefer not to be photographed.
