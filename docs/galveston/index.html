Galveston

Victorian mansions, the Strand and Gulf seafood.

Galveston was once Texas’s richest city, and its Victorian mansions and the iron-front buildings of the Strand still show it. Walk the historic district, tour the 1892 Bishop’s Palace, climb aboard the 1877 tall ship Elissa, ride a bike along the seawall and eat seafood at a restaurant that has been on the beach since 1911.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on the island.

KGLS, Scholes International at Galveston, is a towered airport on the island, about 10 minutes from the Strand and the seawall.

The FBO

Island Jet Center

2115 Terminal Drive, Galveston, TX 77554

409-572-2555

Directories list two FBO names here, Island Jet Center and Galveston Aviation Services; confirm the current operator, hours and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Houston’s Class B airspace lies to the north. Summer brings afternoon storms, and hurricane season runs June to November. About 860 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

A car or the trolley.

The Strand and downtown are walkable; a car or the island trolley helps for the seawall and beaches.
