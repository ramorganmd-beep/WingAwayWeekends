Seaside & 30A

Sugar-white sand and pastel beach towns.

Scenic Highway 30A runs for about 24 miles along the Florida Panhandle through a string of beach towns, each with its own style, from the pastel cottages of Seaside to the white walls of Alys Beach. Bike the path between them, swim in emerald water, walk the dunes around rare coastal lakes and dine looking out over the Gulf.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land 35 minutes from Seaside.

KECP, Northwest Florida Beaches International, is a towered airline airport in Panama City Beach, about 30 minutes from Rosemary Beach and 35 from Seaside.

The FBO

Sheltair

5325 Johnny Reaver Road, Panama City, FL 32409

850-233-4717

Listed hours are 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Southern Sky Aviation is also on the field. Ask about rental cars, parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Tyndall and Eglin Air Force Bases have busy military airspace nearby. Summer brings afternoon storms; hurricane season runs June to November. Review the sectional, restricted areas and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

A car, then bikes.

Rent a car at the airport, then use the 30A bike path between towns. Parking in Seaside and Rosemary Beach fills up on busy days.
