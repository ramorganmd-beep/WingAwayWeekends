Atlantic City

The original boardwalk, resort spas and a classic steakhouse.

Atlantic City has the country’s first boardwalk and some big resort hotels with spas, shows and restaurants. Stay at Borgata or Ocean, dine at the Knife & Fork Inn and Dock’s Oyster House, climb the Absecon Lighthouse and walk the boardwalk at sunset.
