Cedar Key

Old Florida, a short runway by the water and a golf cart.

Cedar Key is a tiny island town on the Gulf coast, with weathered wooden buildings on stilts, working clam farms and some of the best sunsets in Florida. Land on a short runway at the water’s edge, ride a golf cart into town, kayak to a deserted island, eat award-winning clam chowder and watch the sun drop into the Gulf from Dock Street.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at the water’s edge.

KCDK, George T. Lewis Airport, is a small county airport on the island, a short golf-cart ride from town.

The FBO

George T. Lewis Airport (no FBO or fuel)

14703 SW Airport Road, Cedar Key, FL 32625

There is no fuel or FBO. Arrange a golf-cart rental ahead; pilots report carts can meet you at the airport. Tie-downs are available; confirm fees with Levy County.

The single runway is about 2,300 feet, with water close by. Check landing and takeoff performance carefully, especially on hot days, and arrive with plenty of fuel. Watch for birds, gusty sea breezes and afternoon storms in summer.

Getting around

Golf carts and your feet.

The town is tiny. Rent a golf cart or bikes, or simply walk.
