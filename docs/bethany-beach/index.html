Bethany Beach & the Quiet Resorts

Delaware’s quiet beach towns.

Bethany Beach and Fenwick Island are known as the Quiet Resorts: small boardwalks, family-owned restaurants and long uncrowded beaches. Stay oceanfront, walk the boardwalk at sunset, kayak the inland bays, climb the dunes at Delaware Seashore State Park and visit the Fenwick Island lighthouse.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Georgetown.

KGED, Delaware Coastal Airport, is a non-towered field with a 5,500-foot runway, about 35 minutes from Bethany Beach.
