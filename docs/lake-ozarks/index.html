Lake of the Ozarks

A thousand miles of shoreline and a castle on the bluff.

Lake of the Ozarks winds for 92 miles through the Missouri hills, with more shoreline than the California coast. Spend a day on the water, hike to the stone ruins of a 1900s castle above the lake at Ha Ha Tonka, explore a cave, and settle into a lakeside resort for sunset.

Flying with a pilot: what to know

  • Pack light, in soft bags. Small airplanes have weight limits, and your pilot will tell you how much to bring.
  • Dress in layers. It can be cool in the air and warm on the ramp.
  • Plans can shift with the weather: a later start, an earlier trip home or an extra night is part of flying yourselves.
  • Bring water, a snack and sunglasses, and anything you like for motion sickness. Use the restroom before takeoff.
  • Comfortable walking shoes make the weekend.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land above the lake.

KAIZ, Lee C. Fine Memorial, is a city-run airport near the Grand Glaize arm of the lake, about 15 minutes from Osage Beach.

The FBO

City of Osage Beach FBO · Lee C. Fine Memorial

Lee C. Fine Road, Brumley, MO 65017

573-348-5251

Listed hours vary by season. A recent pilot report says the field was out of service for a stretch in 2025, so call ahead to confirm status, fuel, ground transport and parking. No live fuel price is shown.

Rolling, wooded hills and the lake surround the field. About 710 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and consider a third night. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Seaplane access

Tan-Tar-A Resort (19T), public-use seaplane base

Osage Beach, MO · 573-348-8648

573-348-8648

Water lanes: NW/SE about 13,000 × 1,200 ft (measured from a USGS map; no buoys mark the landing area). Mogas listed.

Aircraft may dock at the resort’s boat slips; call first. Heavy boat traffic and rough water April to October; breakwater and buoys across the cove mouth; trees and bluffs all around. (FAA chart supplement remarks.)

The resort is now Margaritaville Lake Resort; confirm current seaplane arrangements.

Water landing rules, conditions and docking change; call the base and check the chart supplement before you go.

Getting around

A car, then a boat.

Resorts and restaurants are spread around the lake; many are easiest to reach by water. Rent a boat for a day.
