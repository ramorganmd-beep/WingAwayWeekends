Tennessee · Couples · Two nights

Knoxville

Fly in where Cirrus delivers its airplanes, then head downtown.

McGhee Tyson Airport is home to Cirrus’s Vision Center, where the company delivers airplanes and trains and supports owners. From there it is about 25 minutes to Market Square and Gay Street. Stay downtown, eat at J.C. Holdway, walk World’s Fair Park to the Sunsphere and hike the Urban Wilderness at Ijams. The Great Smoky Mountains are about an hour away.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at McGhee Tyson.

KTYS, McGhee Tyson Airport, is a towered airport in Class C airspace with two long runways (05L/23R, 10,000 ft, and 05R/23L, 9,000 ft), about 25 minutes south of downtown. Cirrus’s Vision Center (sales, delivery, training and service) is on the field. For an airport minutes from downtown, Knoxville Downtown Island Airport (KDKX, non-towered, 3,499 ft runway) sits on the Tennessee River.

Getting around

Rental car or Uber.

Rent a car or take an Uber the 25 minutes downtown; downtown, Market Square, the Old City and World’s Fair Park are walkable. A car also makes the Smokies an easy day trip.

08 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

Lodging first (check the football schedule), then J.C. Holdway and a rental car.

Two airports

KTYS has the long runways, the tower and the Cirrus Vision Center; KDKX (3,499 ft) is closer to downtown.

Mountain flying

About 280 nm from Myrtle Beach over the Appalachians: check winds, ceilings and terrain.

Cirrus owners

The Vision Center is on the field at KTYS; contact Cirrus ahead about a visit.
