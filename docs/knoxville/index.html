Knoxville

Land on the river, minutes from Market Square.

Knoxville’s Downtown Island Airport sits on the Tennessee River a few minutes from Market Square and Gay Street. Stay downtown, eat at J.C. Holdway, walk World’s Fair Park to the Sunsphere and hike the Urban Wilderness at Ijams. The Great Smoky Mountains are about an hour away, and Cirrus’s Vision Center is across town at McGhee Tyson.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on the river.

KDKX, Knoxville Downtown Island Airport, is a non-towered field on an island in the Tennessee River with one paved runway (08/26, 3,499 ft), a few minutes from downtown. For a longer runway, a tower or the Cirrus Vision Center, use McGhee Tyson (KTYS, 9,000 and 10,000 ft) about 25 minutes south.

The FBO

Mind the 3,499 ft runway and the river and ridgelines around it; check density altitude and your performance numbers. About 280 nm from Myrtle Beach, roughly two hours over the mountains.

Getting around

Uber and walking.

Take an Uber from the airport; downtown, Market Square, the Old City and World’s Fair Park are walkable.
