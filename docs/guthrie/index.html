Guthrie

Oklahoma’s Victorian first capital.

Guthrie grew from nothing to a town of 10,000 on the day of the 1889 Land Run and served as Oklahoma’s capital until 1910. Its downtown is one of the largest historic districts in the country, full of red-brick Victorian buildings. Land at Guthrie-Edmond Regional, stay at the Stone Lion Inn, tour the Scottish Rite Temple and the Territorial Museum, see a show at the Pollard Theatre, and get coffee at Hoboken Coffee Roasters.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A regional airport near town.

KGOK, Guthrie-Edmond Regional, is a non-towered airport at 1,069 feet with a 5,201-foot runway, about 10 minutes from downtown Guthrie.

The FBO

Apex Aero Center

Guthrie-Edmond Regional Airport, Guthrie, OK

405-282-4250

Flight-school traffic is common; Oklahoma City’s airspace is to the south. No live fuel price is shown.

Great Plains flying: strong, gusty winds; spring severe weather with hail and tornadoes from April to June; hot, bumpy summer afternoons; and winter icing. About 920 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.

Getting around

Car or ride.

Downtown is walkable; arrange a ride or a car from the airport.
