Saugatuck & Holland

An artsy lake town with dunes, galleries and sunsets.

Saugatuck is a small art colony where the Kalamazoo River meets Lake Michigan. Walk to Oval Beach, ride the dunes, cruise the river on a paddlewheeler, browse galleries and stay at the Wickwood Inn, then visit nearby Holland for its windmill and, in May, millions of tulips.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Holland.

KBIV, West Michigan Regional in Holland, is a non-towered field about 20 minutes from Saugatuck.

Great Lakes weather: lake-effect clouds and snow from late fall through winter, summer thunderstorms and fog over cold water in spring. About 640 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.
