The Homestead

A grand resort and the warm springs.

Deep in the Allegheny Mountains, Hot Springs has welcomed travelers to its mineral waters since the 1700s. Land on a mountaintop runway, stay at the grand Homestead, soak in the historic Warm Springs Pools, play one of the great mountain golf courses, and dress for dinner.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on a mountaintop.

KHSP, Ingalls Field, sits on a ridge near 3,800 feet, one of the highest airports in the East, about 15–20 minutes from The Homestead.

The FBO

Ingalls Field · Bath County Airport Authority

6240 Airport Road, Hot Springs, VA 24445

540-839-5326

Listed hours are about 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., with a callout fee after hours. Ask the resort about a transfer when you book, and ask the FBO about overnight parking, tie-downs and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

This is serious mountain flying: a ridge-top runway with drop-offs, high density altitude in summer, gusty winds and clouds that can sit on the field. Review the airport diagram, performance, terrain and current NOTAMs, and have an alternate in the valley.

Getting around

The resort does the driving.

Once you’re at The Homestead, most of the weekend is on foot. A car helps for the Warm Springs Pools, Garth Newel and Falling Spring Falls, all within about 15 minutes.

04 / Things to do

Warm water and cool mountains.

Slow down. This is a place for long soaks and long walks.

01

The Warm Springs Pools

Two historic bathhouses over natural warm springs, restored and reopened in 2022. Book a 50-minute soak ahead; you don’t need to stay at the resort.

Pools and reservations
02

Golf on the Cascades course

A classic mountain course, long ranked among the best in Virginia, plus The Old Course at the resort, home to one of the oldest first tees in continuous use in the country.

03

Resort activities

Falconry, fly-fishing, sporting clays, horseback riding, hiking and a zip line. Book a few the week before.

04

Garth Newel Music Center

Chamber music on a farm between Hot Springs and Warm Springs, sometimes with dinner after the concert. Check the season’s schedule.

Concert schedule
05

Falling Spring Falls

A tall roadside waterfall a short drive south of Hot Springs, with an overlook just off the highway.
