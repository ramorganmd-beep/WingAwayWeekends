Okoboji & the Iowa Great Lakes

A clear blue lake and old-fashioned summer fun.

West Okoboji is one of only a handful of blue-water lakes in the world, spring-fed and remarkably clear. Stay at a lakeside resort, cruise on the Queen II, ride the 1930 wooden roller coaster at Arnolds Park, see a show at the Okoboji summer theatre and eat by the water.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Spencer.

KSPW, Spencer Municipal, is a non-towered field with a 6,000-foot runway, about 25 minutes from the lakes.

The FBO

Leading Edge Aviation

Spencer Municipal Airport, Spencer, IA

712-262-1000

Spirit Lake (0F3) is closer but has only a 3,000-by-50-foot runway and no services. Confirm fuel and a car. No live fuel price is shown.

Midwest weather: summer thunderstorms and haze, gusty winds on the plains, and icing and low ceilings from late fall through winter. About 950 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.
