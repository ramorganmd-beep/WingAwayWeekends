Kansas City

Barbecue, jazz and fountains, from a downtown airport.

Kansas City is a barbecue capital and a jazz town, and its downtown airport sits just across the river from the skyline. Visit the TWA Museum at the airport, see the giant shuttlecocks at the Nelson-Atkins, spend a morning at the National WWI Museum, hear jazz at 18th & Vine and eat burnt ends at Joe’s.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land downtown.

KMKC, Charles B. Wheeler Downtown Airport, is a towered airport across the Missouri River from downtown, about 5 minutes from the skyline. The TWA Museum is on the field.

The FBO

Atlantic Aviation

Charles B. Wheeler Downtown Airport, Kansas City, MO

816-949-8900

A second FBO operates on the field (816-842-8484). Confirm hours, fees and cars; 100LL availability varies by FBO. No live fuel price is shown.

Brief the Kansas City Class B. About 825 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan one or two fuel stops and three nights.

Getting around

Streetcar or car.

The free KC Streetcar runs from River Market through downtown to Union Station; a car or rideshare covers the Plaza and barbecue spots.
