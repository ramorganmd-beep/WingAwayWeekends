New Hope & Bucks County

A river arts town, historic inns and Washington’s crossing.

New Hope is a lively arts town on the Delaware River, joined by a footbridge to Lambertville, New Jersey. See a show at the Bucks County Playhouse, ride the steam train, walk the canal towpath, visit Washington Crossing Historic Park and the concrete castles of Doylestown’s Mercer Museum and Fonthill.
