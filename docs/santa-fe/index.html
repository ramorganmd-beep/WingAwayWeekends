Santa Fe

Adobe, art and chile in the high desert.

Santa Fe was founded more than 400 years ago and still feels unlike anywhere else in the country: low adobe buildings, turquoise doors, piñon smoke and a sky that goes on forever. Land at the regional airport south of town, stay on the Plaza, walk the galleries on Canyon Road, see Georgia O’Keeffe’s paintings, and argue about red or green.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A high-desert regional airport.

KSAF, Santa Fe Regional, is a towered field at 6,349 feet with an 8,366-foot main runway about 20 minutes southwest of the Plaza.

The FBO

Signature Aviation Santa Fe

Santa Fe Regional Airport, Santa Fe, NM

505-471-2525

High elevation: compute takeoff performance carefully, especially on summer afternoons. Confirm a rental car or ride. No live fuel price is shown.

High desert flying: hot afternoons with high density altitude, strong gusty winds and dust in spring, summer monsoon storms from July to September, and few alternates. About 1340 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Rental car or taxi.

The Plaza and Canyon Road are walkable; a car helps for the spa, Meow Wolf and day trips.
