Tulsa

Art deco, Route 66 and a surprising music scene.

Oil money built Tulsa into an art deco showcase, and it has never looked better. Stay at the restored 1925 Mayo Hotel, wander the Gathering Place along the Arkansas River, tour Philbrook’s villa and gardens, see the Bob Dylan and Woody Guthrie centers in the Arts District, and catch a show at Cain’s Ballroom, the “Carnegie Hall of Western swing.”

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Riverside.

KRVS, Tulsa Riverside (Richard L. Jones Jr.), is a towered GA airport about 15 minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Christiansen Aviation

Tulsa Riverside Airport, Tulsa, OK

918-298-6650

Riverside Jet Center is another FBO on the field. Confirm hours, fees and cars. No live fuel price is shown.

Plains weather: strong winds, spring and summer thunderstorms (this is tornado country), summer heat and density altitude, and occasional winter ice storms. About 850 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Rent a car or rideshare.

Downtown and the Arts District are walkable; rideshare to the Gathering Place and Philbrook.
