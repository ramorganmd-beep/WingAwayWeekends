Plymouth

America’s Hometown: the Mayflower II, the harbor and a spa in the pines.

Plymouth sits on Cape Cod Bay between Boston and the Cape, where the Pilgrims landed in 1620. Walk the waterfront from Plymouth Rock to the Mayflower II, spend a morning at Plimoth Patuxet, eat seafood on the harbor and finish at a spa resort in The Pinehills.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in Plymouth.

KPYM, Plymouth Municipal Airport, is a non-towered field with two paved runways (06/24, 4,650 ft, and 15/33, 4,350 ft), about 5 miles from the waterfront. Plane Jane’s Place, a breakfast-and-lunch diner, is on the field.

The FBO

Plymouth Municipal Airport

246 South Meadow Road, Plymouth, MA · 100LL and Jet A

508-746-2020FBO details

Watch for coastal fog and low ceilings off Cape Cod Bay. About 630 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and two or three nights.

Getting around

Uber or a rental car.

Downtown and the waterfront are walkable; take an Uber or a rental car from the airport and to Plimoth Patuxet or The Pinehills.
