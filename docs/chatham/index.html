Chatham & Cape Cod

Classic Cape Cod: seals, a lighthouse and a grand beach resort.

Chatham sits at the elbow of Cape Cod, with a Main Street of shops and shingled cottages, a lighthouse above the beach and gray seals hauled out on the sandbars. Stay at Chatham Bars Inn, take a seal tour toward Monomoy, buy fish at the pier and bike the Cape Cod Rail Trail.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in Chatham.

KCQX, Chatham Municipal Airport, is a small non-towered field a few minutes from Main Street.
