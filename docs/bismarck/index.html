Bismarck & Mandan

Lewis and Clark country on the Missouri River.

Bismarck and Mandan face each other across the Missouri, where Lewis and Clark wintered nearby. Spend a morning at the excellent North Dakota Heritage Center & State Museum, tour Custer’s home and the reconstructed Mandan earthlodges at Fort Abraham Lincoln and have dinner at the Pirogue Grille.

Land at Bismarck.

KBIS, Bismarck Municipal, is a towered airport about 10 minutes from downtown.

High plains weather: strong winds, summer thunderstorms and fast-moving winter systems. Check density altitude in summer. About 1260 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.
