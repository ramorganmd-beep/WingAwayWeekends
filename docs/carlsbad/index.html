Carlsbad & LEGOLAND

LEGOLAND, the beach and an airport a few minutes from the park.

McClellan-Palomar Airport sits on the mesa above Carlsbad, a short drive from LEGOLAND California. Stay at a LEGOLAND hotel or next door at the Sheraton with its own walkway into the park, spend a day on the rides and at the SEA LIFE Aquarium, then cool off at Carlsbad State Beach and eat fish tacos in Carlsbad Village. A strong pick for children about 3 to 12.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land a few minutes from the park.

KCRQ, McClellan-Palomar, is a towered airport with one runway, 6/24, 4,897 ft long. Atlantic Aviation and Western Flight serve general aviation; the airport charges a landing fee.

Busy Southern California airspace and noise-sensitive neighborhoods: review the noise procedures. A morning marine layer is common, especially May through July; it usually burns off by late morning.

Getting around

Rental car or Uber.

LEGOLAND, the hotels around it and Carlsbad Village are all a short drive from the airport. Arrange a rental car with child seats through the FBO, or use Uber with your own car seats.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Best ages

LEGOLAND is built for children about 3 to 12. Teens may prefer the beach and San Diego’s bigger parks.

Book in this order

Park tickets and the hotel first, then the rental car with child seats.

Marine layer

Low morning clouds are common on the coast, especially late spring and early summer. Plan late-morning arrivals and departures.

Car seats

Bring your own or reserve them with the rental car; Uber drivers do not supply them.
