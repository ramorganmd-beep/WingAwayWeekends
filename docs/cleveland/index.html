Cleveland

A lakefront landing next to the Rock Hall.

Burke Lakefront Airport sits on Lake Erie right downtown, a short walk from the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame. Spend a day at the free Cleveland Museum of Art and the gardens in University Circle, catch a show at Playhouse Square or the Cleveland Orchestra, graze the West Side Market and have dinner with lake views at Pier W.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on the lakefront.

KBKL, Burke Lakefront, is a towered field on Lake Erie beside downtown, about 5 minutes from the Rock Hall.

The FBO

Signature Aviation

Burke Lakefront Airport, Cleveland, OH

216-861-2030

Confirm FBO hours, fees and ground transportation. No live fuel price is shown.

Great Lakes weather: lake-effect clouds and snow from late fall through winter, summer thunderstorms and fog over cold water in spring. About 490 nm from Myrtle Beach. A long nonstop leg or a fuel stop.

Getting around

Walk or rideshare.

Downtown is walkable; the RTA HealthLine runs to University Circle.
