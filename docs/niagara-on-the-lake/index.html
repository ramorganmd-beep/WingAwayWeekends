Niagara-on-the-Lake

Wine, theater and Niagara Falls.

Niagara-on-the-Lake is a pretty 19th-century town where the Niagara River meets Lake Ontario, surrounded by Ontario’s best-known wine country and famous for icewine. Fly across Lake Erie or along the Niagara River to Niagara District Airport, clear Canadian customs, stay at the Prince of Wales Hotel on Queen Street, see a play at the Shaw Festival, bike between wineries, have dinner at Treadwell, and spend a morning at Niagara Falls.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Niagara District.

CYSN, St. Catharines/Niagara District, is a Canadian airport of entry with a long paved runway, about 10 minutes from Niagara-on-the-Lake.

The FBO

Allied Aviation Services

Niagara District Airport, Niagara-on-the-Lake, ON

905-684-3600

Customs is available; confirm CBSA arrangements and hours with the FBO before you go. Fuel prices are in Canadian dollars per liter, plus taxes. Toronto’s busy airspace is to the north. For the return, Niagara Falls International (KIAG) and Buffalo (KBUF) have U.S. customs; confirm hours and call ahead. Crossing the border: see the Flying to Canada checklist below.

Great Lakes flying: lake-effect snow and low ceilings from November to March, fog off the lakes, summer thunderstorms, and busy Toronto and Buffalo airspace. About 570 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Crossing the border

Flying to Canada

CYSN is a Canadian airport of entry. A trip to Canada takes a little paperwork each way; here is the checklist.

Before you go
  • Passports for everyone aboard, plus your pilot certificate and medical.
  • Radio paperwork: an FCC Restricted Radiotelephone Operator Permit for the pilot and an aircraft radio station license for international flight.
  • Insurance: proof of liability insurance that meets Canadian requirements, carried on board.
  • CBP user-fee decal on the aircraft for the return to the U.S.
  • Flight plan: file one for the border crossing and keep your transponder on with an assigned code.
Flying into Canada
  • File eAPIS at least 60 minutes before leaving the U.S.
  • Call CANPASS at 1-888-226-7277 2 to 48 hours before you arrive, and land at CYSN within your stated arrival window.
  • After landing, follow the CBSA instructions you were given; stay with the aircraft until you are cleared.
Coming home to the U.S.
  • File eAPIS again at least 60 minutes before leaving Canada.
  • Land first at a U.S. airport with customs, such as Niagara Falls International (KIAG) or Buffalo (KBUF). Confirm CBP hours and any advance-notice call before you go.
  • Fuel in Canada is sold by the liter in Canadian dollars, plus taxes.

Rules change; check the current requirements before every trip: AOPA’s Canada guide · CBSA CANPASS · CBP eAPIS
