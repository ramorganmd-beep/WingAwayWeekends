Door County

Harbor villages, cherries and a fish boil.

Door County is a long peninsula between Green Bay and Lake Michigan, lined with small harbor villages, orchards and 11 historic lighthouses. Drive the bay shore through Fish Creek and Ephraim, hike the bluffs of Peninsula State Park, watch a traditional fish boil boil over, and see the goats on the roof at Al Johnson’s.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at the gateway to the peninsula.

KSUE, Door County Cherryland Airport, is just outside Sturgeon Bay, about 30 minutes from Fish Creek and 45 from Ephraim and Sister Bay.

The FBO

Tailwind Flight Center

3538 Park Drive, Sturgeon Bay, WI 54235

920-746-2581

Ask about rental cars, parking, fees and hours. No live fuel price is shown.

Lake effect weather and fog are common, especially in spring and summer. About 780 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and consider a third night. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

You’ll want a car.

The villages are strung along Highway 42 on the bay side and Highway 57 on the lake side. Each is walkable once you park.
