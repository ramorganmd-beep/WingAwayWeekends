Providence

WaterFire, Federal Hill and a great small-city food scene.

Providence is a compact, walkable city with one of New England’s best food scenes. Time a visit for WaterFire, when bonfires burn on the rivers downtown, eat grilled pizza at Al Forno, walk Benefit Street’s Mile of History, see the RISD Museum and dine on Federal Hill.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at T. F. Green.

KPVD, Rhode Island T. F. Green International, is a towered airport about 15 minutes south of downtown.

The FBO

Coastal weather and fog are common. About 600 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Walk downtown.

Downtown, College Hill and Federal Hill are walkable; use a rideshare from the airport.
