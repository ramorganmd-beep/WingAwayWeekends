Napa & Sonoma Wine Country

America’s most famous wine country.

Napa Valley runs 30 miles north from the bay, lined with famous wineries, and the Sonoma Valley lies just over the Mayacamas Mountains with a looser, friendlier feel. Land at Napa County Airport at the south end of the valley, stay in a hillside resort or in Yountville, taste with a driver, ride the Wine Train, eat at Bouchon or the French Laundry and spend an afternoon around Sonoma’s historic plaza.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Napa County.

KAPC, Napa County, is a towered field at 36 feet with a 5,930-foot main runway at the south end of the valley, about 15 minutes from downtown Napa and 25 from Yountville.

The FBO

Atlantic Aviation Napa

Napa County Airport, Napa, CA

707-224-0887

Morning fog from the bay is common in summer. Sonoma County (KSTS) is an alternative for the Sonoma side. Arrange a driver for tasting. No live fuel price is shown.

Northern California coast and Bay Area: summer fog and low stratus pouring through the Golden Gate in the evening and morning, clear fall days, winter Pacific storms, and some of the busiest airspace in the country. About 2110 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Car, driver or wine train.

Book a driver or tour for tasting days; downtown Napa and Yountville are walkable.
