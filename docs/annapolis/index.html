Annapolis

Sailboats, brick streets and the Naval Academy.

Annapolis is a colonial capital on the Chesapeake, with brick streets, a harbor full of sailboats and the U.S. Naval Academy at the end of town. Tour the Academy, see the State House where George Washington resigned his commission, take a sunset sail on a schooner and eat crab cakes on the water.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Lee Airport.

KANP, Lee Airport, is a small non-towered field in Edgewater, about 15 minutes south of downtown Annapolis.

The FBO

Lee Airport

Lee Airport, Edgewater, MD

410-956-1280

Lee Airport sells 100LL. Confirm hours, transient parking and fees before you go; rideshare is the easiest way into town. No live fuel price is shown.

Lee sits under the Washington Class B and near the DC Special Flight Rules Area. Brief the SFRA and Flight Restricted Zone boundaries carefully. About 335 nm from Myrtle Beach.

Getting around

Walk the historic district.

Downtown, the harbor and the Naval Academy are all walkable; use a rideshare from the airport.
