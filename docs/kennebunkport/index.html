Kennebunkport

Lobster rolls, a rocky coast and a famous inn.

Kennebunkport is a classic Maine harbor village of sea captains’ houses, sailboats and lobster boats. Stroll Dock Square, drive Ocean Avenue past the crashing surf and Walker’s Point, order a lobster roll at a harbor shack, and end with a candlelit dinner at a celebrated inn.

Flying with a pilot: what to know

  • Pack light, in soft bags. Small airplanes have weight limits, and your pilot will tell you how much to bring.
  • Dress in layers. It can be cool in the air and warm on the ramp.
  • Plans can shift with the weather: a later start, an earlier trip home or an extra night is part of flying yourselves.
  • Bring water, a snack and sunglasses, and anything you like for motion sickness. Use the restroom before takeoff.
  • Comfortable walking shoes make the weekend.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land 25 minutes from the coast.

KSFM, Sanford Seacoast Regional, is a non-towered airport inland, about 25 minutes from Kennebunkport.

The FBO

Southern Maine Aviation

199 Airport Road, Sanford, ME 04073

207-324-8919

Ask about rental cars, parking, fees and hours. No live fuel price is shown.

Portsmouth’s airspace lies to the south. Coastal fog and low clouds are common in summer. About 700 nm from Myrtle Beach; plan a fuel stop. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

You’ll want a car.

Dock Square and the harbor are walkable once you arrive; a car helps for the beaches, Ocean Avenue and the coast.
