Fredericksburg & the Hill Country

A hangar hotel, German Main Street and Hill Country wine.

Fredericksburg was settled by German immigrants in 1846, and its Main Street still mixes biergartens with boutiques and tasting rooms. Stay at a 1940s-style hotel right on the airport, spend a morning at the National Museum of the Pacific War, taste along Wine Road 290, and watch the sunset from the granite dome of Enchanted Rock.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Taxi to your hotel.

T82, Gillespie County Airport, is about two miles south of downtown, with the Hangar Hotel and its diner right on the field.

The FBO

Fredericksburg FBO

217 Airport Road, Fredericksburg, TX 78624

830-997-3313

Listed hours are 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. daily, with after-hours service for a fee by arrangement. Ask about rental cars, parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

This is a long trip, about 1,040 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan one or two fuel stops and three or four nights. Summer heat raises density altitude; spring brings storms. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

A car for the Hill Country.

Main Street is walkable. Use a car, or a winery shuttle, for Wine Road 290 and Enchanted Rock.

04 / Things to do

History, wine and granite.

Allow half a day for the museum.

01

National Museum of the Pacific War

A large museum of the war in the Pacific in Admiral Nimitz’s hometown, with aircraft, a combat zone and a Japanese garden.

Museum information
02

Wine Road 290

Dozens of wineries between Fredericksburg and Johnson City. Taste on the non-flying day, with a driver or shuttle.

Enchanted Rock State Natural Area

A huge pink granite dome to climb, about 25 minutes north. Reserve day passes ahead.

Main Street boutiques and a Hill Country pantry.
