Aspen

High-country glamour at the end of a demanding approach.

Aspen began as a silver-mining town and became the Rockies’ most famous resort. The airport sits three miles from downtown in a narrow valley at 7,838 feet, so this is a trip for well-prepared pilots. Stay at the 1889 Hotel Jerome or ski-in at the Little Nell, hike to the Maroon Bells, catch a summer concert and eat very well.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A demanding mountain airport.

KASE, Aspen–Pitkin County/Sardy Field, is a towered field at 7,838 feet with an 8,006-foot runway in a narrow valley. Expect high density altitude, mountain wave, steep terrain, a noise curfew and seasonal traffic.

The FBO

Atlantic Aviation Aspen

Aspen–Pitkin County Airport, Aspen, CO

970-920-2016

Read the airport’s pilot procedures and the chart supplement. Many pilots take a mountain checkout first. Confirm parking, which fills in ski season and on holidays. No live fuel price is shown.

Mountain flying: high density altitude on summer afternoons, mountain wave and strong winds over the ridges, afternoon thunderstorms from July to September, and winter snow and icing. Consider a mountain-flying course before you go. About 1380 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Shuttle or taxi.

Downtown is a short ride; the free RFTA buses connect town, Snowmass and the Maroon Bells.
