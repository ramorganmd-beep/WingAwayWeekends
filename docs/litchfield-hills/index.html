Litchfield Hills

Country inns, a lake and village greens.

The Litchfield Hills are rolling, wooded country with white-steepled villages, and they hide some of New England’s most romantic inns. Stay at the Mayflower Inn & Spa or Winvian Farm, dine at Arethusa al tavolo, taste wine at Hopkins Vineyard above Lake Waramaug and walk the trails at White Memorial.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Waterbury–Oxford.

KOXC, Waterbury–Oxford Airport, is a towered field about 35 minutes from Litchfield and Washington.

The FBO

Clay Lacy Aviation

Waterbury–Oxford Airport, Oxford, CT

860-351-7499

Confirm FBO hours, fees and ground transportation. No live fuel price is shown.

New York airspace lies to the south; hilly terrain can bring low ceilings. About 540 nm from Myrtle Beach. A long nonstop leg or a fuel stop.

Getting around

Rent a car.

The inns and villages are spread across the hills; you need a car.
