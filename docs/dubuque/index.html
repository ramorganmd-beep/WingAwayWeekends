Dubuque

Bluffs, a river museum and the Field of Dreams.

Dubuque climbs the bluffs above the Mississippi. Ride the Fenelon Place Elevator up the bluff for the view, spend a morning at the National Mississippi River Museum & Aquarium, stay at the historic Hotel Julien, taste wine in an old brewery and drive 30 minutes to play catch at the Field of Dreams.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Dubuque.

KDBQ, Dubuque Regional, is a towered airport about 15 minutes south of downtown.

The FBO

Dubuque Jet Center

Dubuque Regional Airport, Dubuque, IA

563-589-4136

Midwest weather: summer thunderstorms and haze, gusty winds on the plains, and icing and low ceilings from late fall through winter. About 760 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.
