South Padre Island

Texas’s tropical tip: beaches, turtles and dolphins.

At the southern tip of Texas, South Padre Island has warm water, wide beaches and a laid-back, almost tropical feel. Meet rescued sea turtles, watch dolphins in the Laguna Madre, climb the 1852 Port Isabel Lighthouse and eat fresh Gulf shrimp on the bay at sunset.

Flying with a pilot: what to know

  • Pack light, in soft bags. Small airplanes have weight limits, and your pilot will tell you how much to bring.
  • Dress in layers. It can be cool in the air and warm on the ramp.
  • Plans can shift with the weather: a later start, an earlier trip home or an extra night is part of flying yourselves.
  • Bring water, a snack and sunglasses, and anything you like for motion sickness. Use the restroom before takeoff.
  • Comfortable walking shoes make the weekend.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on the mainland, 15 minutes from the island.

KPIL, Port Isabel–Cameron County Airport, is a non-towered field on the mainland, about 15 minutes from the Queen Isabella Causeway and the island.

The FBO

Southwind Airlines (FBO) · Port Isabel–Cameron County Airport

Port Isabel–Cameron County Airport, Los Fresnos, TX 78566

956-559-6677

Sources give different FBO phone numbers, so confirm. Listed hours are 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Arrange a rental car ahead. No live fuel price is shown.

SpaceX’s Starbase at Boca Chica is nearby; launches bring TFRs and closures, so check NOTAMs closely. The Mexican border is close; brief the ADIZ and airspace. About 1,060 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan one or two fuel stops and three or four nights.

Getting around

A car, then flip-flops.

The island is small and easy to explore; a car helps for the causeway and Port Isabel.
