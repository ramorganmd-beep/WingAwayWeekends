Chicago

Architecture, art and great food in America’s big Midwest city.

Chicago is the Midwest’s grand weekend. Take the Chicago Architecture Center river cruise, spend an afternoon at the Art Institute and Millennium Park, catch a game at Wrigley Field, eat deep-dish and splurge on a tasting menu, then stay in a landmark hotel off Michigan Avenue.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Chicago Executive.

KPWK, Chicago Executive Airport, is a towered GA airport in Wheeling, about 45 minutes from downtown by car. It avoids O’Hare and Midway.

The FBO

Atlantic Aviation

Chicago Executive Airport, Wheeling, IL

847-808-0812

Confirm FBO hours, fees and ground transportation. No live fuel price is shown.

Great Lakes weather: lake-effect clouds and snow from late fall through winter, summer thunderstorms and fog over cold water in spring. About 660 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Rideshare or train.

Downtown is walkable with the L; rideshare from the airport.
