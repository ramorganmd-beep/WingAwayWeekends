Wichita

The Air Capital of the World.

Wichita built more airplanes than anywhere else, from Cessna and Beech to Learjet, and pilots will love it. Tour the Kansas Aviation Museum in the 1935 terminal, see the Keeper of the Plains lit by fire at the rivers’ confluence, walk Botanica’s gardens and stay in the restored warehouses of Old Town.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in the Air Capital.

KICT, Wichita Dwight D. Eisenhower National, is a towered airport about 15 minutes from downtown. Several GA airports surround the city.

The FBO

Yingling Aviation

Wichita Eisenhower National Airport, Wichita, KS

316-943-3246

Signature and Clemens Aviation are also on the field. Confirm hours, fees and cars. No live fuel price is shown.

Plains weather: strong winds, spring and summer thunderstorms (this is tornado country) and fast-moving winter systems. About 930 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.

Getting around

Rent a car.

Old Town is walkable; a car covers the museums.
