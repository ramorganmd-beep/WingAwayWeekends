Camden & Midcoast Maine

Windjammers, a harbor town and mountains meeting the sea.

Camden is the picture of coastal Maine: a harbor full of windjammers, white houses and mountains rising behind the town. Drive up Mount Battie for the view, sail on a schooner, see the Wyeths at Rockland’s Farnsworth Art Museum, eat lobster by the water and visit the Owls Head Transportation Museum, right next to the airport.

Flying with a pilot: what to know

  • Pack light, in soft bags. Small airplanes have weight limits, and your pilot will tell you how much to bring.
  • Dress in layers. It can be cool in the air and warm on the ramp.
  • Plans can shift with the weather: a later start, an earlier trip home or an extra night is part of flying yourselves.
  • Bring water, a snack and sunglasses, and anything you like for motion sickness. Use the restroom before takeoff.
  • Comfortable walking shoes make the weekend.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Knox County.

KRKD, Knox County Regional, is in Owls Head, about 20 minutes from Camden and 10 from Rockland. The Owls Head Transportation Museum is on the field.

The FBO

Downeast Air

Knox County Regional Airport, Owls Head, ME

207-594-2171

Confirm FBO hours, fees and ground transportation. No live fuel price is shown.

Coastal fog is common in summer, especially in the morning. About 770 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.
