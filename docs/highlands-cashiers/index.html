Highlands–Cashiers

Waterfalls, cool air and a great inn.

High on a plateau in the Blue Ridge, the twin villages of Highlands and Cashiers are cooler than the valleys below and surrounded by waterfalls and granite cliffs. Stay at a polished mountain inn, walk behind a waterfall, hike to the cliffs of Whiteside Mountain, and end with dinner on a village main street.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in the valley, drive up the mountain.

K1A5, Macon County Airport in Franklin, is about 35–40 minutes from Highlands by a winding mountain road; Cashiers is about 20 minutes farther. Jackson County Airport (24A) near Sylva is an alternative for Cashiers.

The FBO

Macon Air · Macon County Airport

1241 Airport Road, Franklin, NC 28734

828-524-5529

The FBO lists a courtesy car and rental cars; for two nights, reserve a rental car ahead. CTAF 123.05. Ask about parking, fees and hours. No live fuel price is shown.

This is mountain flying: high terrain surrounds the valley, and clouds and fog can linger on the ridges. Review terrain, density altitude, the sectional and current NOTAMs, and plan morning flights in summer.

Getting around

You’ll want a car.

The road up to Highlands passes waterfalls and steep curves; allow extra time and drive it in daylight. Both villages are walkable once you arrive.

04 / Things to do

Waterfalls and granite cliffs.

Most of the best sights are short walks from the road.

01

Dry Falls & Bridal Veil Falls

On the road between Franklin and Highlands: walk behind the curtain of Dry Falls, then pass Bridal Veil Falls, which spills right beside the highway.

02

Whiteside Mountain

A two-mile loop to sheer granite cliffs with long views over the Blue Ridge. Moderate, and one of the area’s classic hikes.

03

Sunset Rock

A short walk from downtown Highlands to a rock ledge with a view over the town and mountains. Go for sunset.

04

Whitewater Falls

Among the highest waterfalls in the eastern United States, about 30 minutes south of Cashiers, with a short paved walk to the overlook.

05

Main Street Highlands & Cashiers

Galleries, boutiques and wine shops in two small villages. An easy afternoon.

Optional / Spa & relaxation

The spa at Old Edwards · Time for two

Old Edwards Inn’s spa is one of the best known in the Southeast, with couples treatments, a heated mineral pool and quiet lounges.

08 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

Lodging and a car first, then dinners and the spa.

Mountain flying

High terrain, valley fog and quick-building storms. Fly in the morning and keep a backup day.

Mountain roads

The road from Franklin is steep and winding. Drive it in daylight.

Peak seasons

Summer weekends and October fill early. Book well ahead.
