Sioux Falls

Waterfalls downtown and a lively small city.

Sioux Falls is built around its namesake falls, cascading over pink quartzite in the middle of town. Walk Falls Park, browse the outdoor SculptureWalk along Phillips Avenue, stay at the Hotel on Phillips and have dinner at Parker’s Bistro.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Sioux Falls.

KFSD, Sioux Falls Regional (Joe Foss Field), is a towered airport about 10 minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Maverick Air Center

Sioux Falls Regional Airport, Sioux Falls, SD

605-312-5680

Confirm FBO hours, fees and ground transportation. No live fuel price is shown.

High plains weather: strong winds, summer thunderstorms and fast-moving winter systems. Check density altitude in summer. About 1020 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.
