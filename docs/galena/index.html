Galena

A 19th-century river town in the rolling hills.

Galena was a booming lead-mining and steamboat town in the 1850s, and most of its brick downtown survives. Stroll a mile of shops and restaurants on Main Street, tour the house the town gave Ulysses S. Grant after the Civil War, drive the hills to a winery and watch the Mississippi from the bluffs.

Flying with a pilot: what to know

  • Pack light, in soft bags. Small airplanes have weight limits, and your pilot will tell you how much to bring.
  • Dress in layers. It can be cool in the air and warm on the ramp.
  • Plans can shift with the weather: a later start, an earlier trip home or an extra night is part of flying yourselves.
  • Bring water, a snack and sunglasses, and anything you like for motion sickness. Use the restroom before takeoff.
  • Comfortable walking shoes make the weekend.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land across the river in Iowa.

KDBQ, Dubuque Regional, is a towered airport in Iowa, about 30 minutes from Galena across the Mississippi.

The FBO

Dubuque Jet Center

11000 Airport Road, Dubuque, IA 52003

563-589-4136

Ask about rental cars, parking, fees and hours. No live fuel price is shown.

The Mississippi River valley can bring fog and gusty winds. About 760 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and consider a third night. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

A car, then walk.

Rent a car at Dubuque. Downtown Galena is walkable; the hills, wineries and Eagle Ridge need a car.
