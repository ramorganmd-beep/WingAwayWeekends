Scottsdale

Resorts, golf and the Sonoran Desert.

Scottsdale is Arizona’s classic resort town, with spa resorts below Camelback Mountain, a walkable Old Town of galleries and restaurants, and the saguaro-covered Sonoran Desert all around. Land at Scottsdale Airport, stay at the Phoenician or mid-century Hotel Valley Ho, tour Frank Lloyd Wright’s desert home and school at Taliesin West, walk the Desert Botanical Garden, and plan a winter visit around spring training or the Barrett-Jackson car auction.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Scottsdale.

KSDL, Scottsdale Airport, is a towered airport at 1,510 feet with an 8,249-foot runway, inside the Phoenix Class B and about 15 minutes from Old Town.

The FBO

Jet Aviation Scottsdale

Scottsdale Airport, Scottsdale, AZ

480-866-8270

Other FBOs: Atlantic Aviation (480-948-2400) and Signature Aviation (480-951-2525). Confirm parking ahead, especially during Barrett-Jackson and the Phoenix Open, when the ramp fills. Under the Phoenix Class B; voluntary noise-abatement curfew 10 p.m. to 6 a.m. Transient overnight parking fees apply. No live fuel price is shown.

Desert Southwest flying: summer temperatures above 110°F with high density altitude, strong afternoon winds and turbulence, monsoon storms and dust from July to September, and long distances between airports. About 1640 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Rideshare or car.

Rideshare works for Old Town and the resorts; rent a car for Taliesin West, the garden and desert drives.
