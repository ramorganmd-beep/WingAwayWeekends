Put-in-Bay & the Lake Erie Islands

Fly straight onto a Lake Erie island and explore by golf cart.

Put-in-Bay is a classic fly-in: the airport is on South Bass Island, a short cart ride from the harbor. Climb Perry’s Victory and International Peace Memorial, taste island wine and tour the crystal cave at Heineman’s, eat lobster bisque on the waterfront and watch the sun set over the lake.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on the island.

3W2, Put-in-Bay Airport, is a non-towered field on South Bass Island with a 2,870-foot runway. There is no FBO; check the airport’s current notes for parking, fees and fuel before you go.

The FBO

Erie Island Carts (cart rentals at the airport)

Put-in-Bay Airport, South Bass Island, OH

419-285-5278

No FBO and no live fuel price. Arrive with fuel for the return or plan a mainland stop. Short runway: check performance and winds.

Great Lakes weather: lake-effect clouds and snow from late fall through winter, summer thunderstorms and fog over cold water in spring. About 510 nm from Myrtle Beach. A long nonstop leg or a fuel stop.

Getting around

Rent a golf cart.

Carts and bikes cover the whole island; downtown is walkable.
