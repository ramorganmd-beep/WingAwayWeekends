Worth the flight

Flight-time estimates are illustrative, not operational flight plans. They use straight-line distance and either your aircraft profile (cruise speed, fuel burn and climb/descent/taxi allowance) or a default 150 KTAS plus 15 minutes, without wind, routing, altitude or ATC delays. Fuel figures and “fuel stop likely” flags are rough planning aids only. Confirm aircraft performance, current airport information, weather, NOTAMs, fuel and all flight-planning details using authoritative sources and your normal tools (such as ForeFlight). Destination listings and recommendations are beta content, not verified live availability.
