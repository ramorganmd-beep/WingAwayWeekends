Naples

Gulf sunsets, a historic pier and Fifth Avenue.

Naples is a polished Gulf Coast town of white-sand beaches, palm-lined streets and spectacular sunsets. Watch the sun set from the 1888 Naples Pier, stroll Fifth Avenue South and Third Street for dinner, wander the Naples Botanical Garden, and walk the boardwalk through an ancient cypress swamp.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land five minutes from downtown.

KAPF, Naples Municipal, is a towered airport a few minutes from Fifth Avenue South and the beaches.

The FBO

Naples Aviation

160 Aviation Drive North, Naples, FL 34104

239-643-0404

Listed hours are 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. Naples Jet Center is also on the field. Naples has noise-abatement procedures; review them before you go. Ask about rental cars, parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Fort Myers’ Class C airspace lies to the north. Summer brings afternoon storms; hurricane season runs June to November. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

A car, then walk.

Downtown, the pier and Fifth Avenue are close together; a car helps for the garden and the swamp.
