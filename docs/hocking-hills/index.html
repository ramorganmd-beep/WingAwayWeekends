Hocking Hills

Waterfalls, gorges and cozy cabins in the Ohio hills.

Hocking Hills is Ohio’s favorite romantic escape: hemlock gorges, waterfalls and recess caves, with cabins and inns tucked into the woods. Hike Old Man’s Cave, Ash Cave and Cedar Falls, zip-line the canopy, book a couples massage and finish with dinner by the fire.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Lancaster.

KLHQ, Fairfield County Airport, is a non-towered field with a 5,000-foot runway, about 35 minutes from the state park.
