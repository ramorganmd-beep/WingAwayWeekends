Sedona

Land on a mesa in red-rock country.

Sedona’s airport sits on top of a mesa in the middle of town, with red-rock buttes in every direction. Land, have lunch at the Mesa Grill by the runway, and stay at a creekside or canyon resort. Hike to Cathedral Rock, take a Jeep tour, wander Tlaquepaque’s courtyards and watch the rocks glow at sunset.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A mesa-top runway with a view.

KSEZ, Sedona, is a non-towered field at 4,830 feet with a 5,132-foot runway on top of a mesa, with drop-offs at both ends.

The FBO

Red Rock Aviation (Sedona Airport)

Sedona Airport, Sedona, AZ

928-282-1046

Expect wind shear and turbulence near the mesa edges, high density altitude in summer and busy tour traffic. Read the airport’s procedures. No live fuel price is shown.

High desert flying: hot afternoons with high density altitude, strong gusty winds and dust in spring, summer monsoon storms from July to September, and few alternates. About 1620 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Car, taxi or shuttle.

Uptown and Tlaquepaque are walkable from many hotels; a car helps for trailheads.
