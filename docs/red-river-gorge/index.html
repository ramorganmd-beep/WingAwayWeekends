Red River Gorge & Natural Bridge

Arches, cliffs and a lodge in the woods.

The Red River Gorge in the Daniel Boone National Forest has one of the largest concentrations of natural arches east of the Rockies, and Natural Bridge State Resort Park sits at its edge. Land at Mount Sterling, about 40 minutes away, stay at the Hemlock Lodge beneath the bridge, hike to Sky Bridge and Chimney Top, and eat pizza where the climbers do.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Mount Sterling.

KIOB, Mount Sterling–Montgomery County, is a non-towered field with a 5,000-foot runway about 40 minutes northwest of the gorge.

The FBO

Kentucky Airmotive

Mount Sterling–Montgomery County Airport, Mount Sterling, KY

859-498-1000

Arrange a rental car or ride before you go; there is little transport near the gorge. No live fuel price is shown.

Kentucky weather: summer haze and afternoon thunderstorms, valley fog on fall mornings, and low ceilings and icing in winter. About 360 nm from Myrtle Beach.

Getting around

Car.

A car is essential; trailheads are spread along winding roads.
