Moab, Arches & Canyonlands

Red rock, two national parks and big skies.

Moab sits in a red-rock valley on the Colorado River, with Arches National Park on the edge of town and Canyonlands a short drive away. Land at Canyonlands Regional north of town, see Delicate Arch at sunset, stand on the rim at Island in the Sky and Dead Horse Point, float the river and stay at a ranch on its banks. Scenic flights over the canyons are a highlight.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A desert regional airport.

KCNY, Canyonlands Regional, is a non-towered field at 4,579 feet with a 7,360-foot runway about 16 miles north of Moab, a few minutes from the Arches entrance road.

The FBO

Redtail Aviation

Canyonlands Regional Airport, Moab, UT

435-259-7421

Hot afternoons: compute performance for the temperature. Book a rental car in advance; there are few. No live fuel price is shown.

High desert flying: hot afternoons with high density altitude, strong gusty winds and dust in spring, summer monsoon storms from July to September, and few alternates. About 1520 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Rental car.

A car is essential for the parks; downtown Moab is walkable.
