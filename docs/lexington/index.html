Lexington & Horse Country

Keeneland, horse farms and bourbon.

Lexington’s airport sits across the road from Keeneland, one of the world’s most beautiful racetracks. Watch the morning workouts, visit retired champions at Old Friends, tour Woodford Reserve, drive the stone-fenced lanes of horse country, spend a day at Shaker Village and have dinner at Holly Hill Inn.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land across from Keeneland.

KLEX, Blue Grass Airport, is a towered airport directly across Versailles Road from Keeneland and about 15 minutes from downtown.
