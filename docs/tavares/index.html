Tavares & Mount Dora

America’s Seaplane City and a lakeside village.

Tavares calls itself America’s Seaplane City, and its seaplane base sits right downtown at Wooton Park on Lake Dora. Splash in for lunch, then drive ten minutes to Mount Dora, a hilly village of antique shops and oaks where the 1883 Lakeside Inn is Florida’s oldest continuously operating hotel. Pilots chasing a seaplane rating can add a day at Jack Brown’s in Winter Haven.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Leesburg or on Lake Dora.

KLEE, Leesburg International, is a towered airport about 15 minutes from Tavares. Floatplanes and amphibians can land at FA1, the city-owned Tavares Seaplane Base and marina downtown.

Florida weather: daily summer thunderstorms by mid-afternoon, low morning fog in winter, and hurricane season from June to November. About 330 nm from Myrtle Beach.

Seaplane access

Water lanes: 09W/27W 3,000 × 200 ft on Lake Dora; 100LL and mogas listed.

VFR operations. Be aware of Leesburg airport traffic to the northwest. (FAA chart supplement remarks.)

Jack Brown’s Seaplane Base (F57, Winter Haven) is about 50 nm south, the best-known place to earn a seaplane rating.

Water landing rules, conditions and docking change; call the base and check the chart supplement before you go.
