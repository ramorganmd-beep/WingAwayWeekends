Lake Lanier

A day on the water, a dinner downtown.

Lake Lanier spreads across the North Georgia foothills with hundreds of miles of shoreline. Land in Gainesville beside the lake, spend a day on a pontoon, have dinner on Gainesville’s historic square, and, if you like, add a wine afternoon in Dahlonega, half an hour away.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land beside the lake.

KGVL, Lee Gilmer Memorial in Gainesville, sits near the lake’s north end, minutes from downtown Gainesville and about 30 minutes from Lanier Islands.

The FBO

Champion Aviation Services

1137 Aviation Way, Gainesville, GA 30501

770-532-4136

Recent pilot reports say there is no courtesy car, so reserve a rental car and confirm pickup at the airport. A second operator on the field, Lanier Flight Center, also sells fuel. Ask about two-night parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Atlanta’s Class B airspace lies to the southwest. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs, and expect afternoon storms in summer.

Getting around

Car on land, pontoon on the lake.

Rent a car for the weekend. For the lake, rent a pontoon by the hour or day; several companies deliver to docks or rent from Lanier Islands.

04 / Things to do

Mostly, the water.

Spend one day on the lake and one exploring.

01

A day on a pontoon

Rent a pontoon and find a quiet cove for a swim and a picnic. Hundreds of miles of wooded shoreline make it easy to escape the crowds. Lanier Luxury Boat Rentals is one well-reviewed option.

Lanier Luxury Boat Rentals
02

Lanier Islands

The resort islands at the lake’s south end have a beach, golf, a spa and boat rentals. Easy for an afternoon even if you stay elsewhere.

03

Lake Lanier Olympic Park

The rowing and canoe venue from the 1996 Atlanta Olympic Games, on the lake in Gainesville. A pretty spot to walk and watch the rowers.

04

Downtown Gainesville

A historic square with restaurants, shops and the Atlanta Botanical Garden’s Gainesville garden nearby.

05

Dahlonega wineries

Half an hour north: a gold-rush square and mountain wineries. Taste on the non-flying day.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

Rental car and lodging first, then a boat and dinners.

No courtesy car

Recent reports say KGVL has none. Reserve a rental car ahead.

Busy summer weekends

The lake is crowded on summer weekends and holidays. Start early.

Boating safety

Wear life jackets and keep a sober skipper; the lake is busy and storms build quickly.
