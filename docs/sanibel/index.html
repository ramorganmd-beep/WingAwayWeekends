Sanibel & Captiva

Shells, wildlife and island sunsets.

Sanibel and Captiva are barrier islands off Fort Myers, famous for some of the best shelling in the world. Bike the island paths, drive or kayak the J.N. “Ding” Darling National Wildlife Refuge, walk the beach doing the “Sanibel stoop,” and watch the sun set over the Gulf from Captiva. The islands are still rebuilding after 2022’s Hurricane Ian, so check that places are open.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Page Field, 40 minutes from the islands.

KFMY, Page Field, is a towered general aviation airport in Fort Myers, about 40 minutes from Sanibel across the causeway.

The FBO

Base Operations at Page Field

5200 Captain Channing Page Drive, Fort Myers, FL 33907

239-590-6600

Self-serve 100LL is listed as available around the clock. Ask about rental cars, parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Southwest Florida International’s Class C airspace lies just to the south. Summer brings afternoon storms; hurricane season runs June to November. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

A car, then bikes.

The Sanibel Causeway has a toll. On the islands, bike paths are the best way around.
