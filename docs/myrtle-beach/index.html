Myrtle Beach

60 miles of beach, the Golf Capital of the World, great seafood and lots of fun things to do.

Myrtle Beach International sits on the old Air Force base at the south end of town, a few minutes from the ocean. Next door at the Market Common, Warbird Park displays an A-10 Warthog, an F-100 and an A-7 from the base’s flying days. Stay at a resort on the ocean, spend an evening on the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk and save a morning for the Boardwalk or a round of golf in the Golf Capital of the World.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land minutes from the beach.

KMYR, Myrtle Beach International, is a towered airport in Class C airspace with a 9,503 ft runway (18/36). The general aviation side is served by Myrtle Beach Aviation. Grand Strand Airport (KCRE) in North Myrtle Beach is a quieter alternative closer to the north end of the beach.

Getting around

Rental car or Uber.

Take an Uber or rent a car for the weekend: the Market Common is next to the airport, the oceanfront is a few minutes away and Murrells Inlet is about 20 minutes south. A car makes the gardens, the MarshWalk and golf easy.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

Lodging first in summer and spring golf season, then dinners on the MarshWalk and a rental car.

Two airports

KMYR has the long runway, the tower and Myrtle Beach Aviation; Grand Strand Airport (KCRE) is closer to North Myrtle Beach.

Summer weather

Afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer; plan morning arrivals and departures.

Beach traffic

Summer weekends crowd Kings Highway and Ocean Boulevard; a car makes the south end and Murrells Inlet easier.
