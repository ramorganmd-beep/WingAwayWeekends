New River Gorge

A national park gorge, a famous bridge and whitewater.

New River Gorge became a national park in 2020, and it is one of the East’s great outdoor weekends. Walk the catwalk beneath the New River Gorge Bridge, raft some of the country’s best whitewater, hike to Long Point for the classic bridge view, see the Glade Creek Grist Mill and eat pizza in the little town of Fayetteville.
