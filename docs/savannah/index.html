09 / Suggested itinerary

Friday in. Sunday home.

A sample Friday–Sunday weekend: more afternoon shopping, one special dinner and one casual seafood evening. No bookings or availability confirmed.

FRI

Arrive & unwind

  1. 10 AM arrival

    Planned arrival at KSAV, subject to the actual flight plan. Transfer to Hyatt Regency Savannah; ask about luggage storage if your room is not ready.

  2. Before dinner

    Browse Broughton Street boutiques after lunch and check in when your room is ready. Keep the afternoon flexible if arrival is delayed.

  3. Evening

    Dinner at The Olde Pink House if reserved. Optional wine stop if arrival leaves enough time.

SAT

Explore & linger

  1. Morning

    Forsyth Park and breakfast or brunch at Collins Quarter. Optional late-morning couples massage at Spa Bleu, if Saturday appointments are offered; adjust meal timing around it rather than rushing. No cathedral visit in this itinerary.

  2. Afternoon

    Give boutique shopping the afternoon: Whitaker Street’s Design District or downtown shops and City Market. Leave room for a café or wine stop and a break at the hotel.

  3. Evening

    Casual riverfront seafood at The Shrimp Factory. A sunset cruise is optional, only if it fits comfortably around dinner; it should not shorten the shopping afternoon.

SUN

An easy departure

  1. Morning

    Breakfast and a short walk. The museum is optional if opening hours and departure timing fit.

  2. Before leaving

    Check out, arrange luggage storage if needed and return to the FBO with ample time for aircraft preparation before the planned 3:30 PM departure.

  3. Fly home

    Let your actual weather and flight plan set the departure, with room to extend or change the trip.

10 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

If a game drives the trip, secure its tickets first. Then choose flexible lodging, anchor dinner reservations and one water outing.

Allow for the season

Summer can be hot and humid. Coastal weather can disrupt flights and boat outings. Check the actual forecast and tour cancellation policies.

Check access & transfers

Confirm stairs, elevator access and room details with lodging. Check the boat boarding point and your return ride before booking an island excursion.

Dates come next

Hotel prices, tickets, hours, drive times and rideshare fares are date-dependent. No availability or live price is implied here.
