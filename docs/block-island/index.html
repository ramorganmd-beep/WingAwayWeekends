Block Island

Bluffs, lighthouses and bikes, a short hop off the coast.

Block Island is 12 miles off the Rhode Island coast, and landing there feels like arriving somewhere far away. Bike past stone walls to Mohegan Bluffs and the Southeast Lighthouse, walk out to North Light, stay at the 1852 Spring House Hotel and watch the sunset over the Great Salt Pond.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on the island.

KBID, Block Island State Airport, is a small non-towered field about a mile from Old Harbor. The runway is short; check performance carefully.

The FBO

Block Island State Airport

Owned by the Rhode Island Airport Corporation (401-691-2227)

No FBO is listed and fuel may not be available; arrive with enough fuel to return to the mainland, and confirm parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Coastal fog can close the field quickly; have a mainland alternate. About 570 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Bikes and mopeds.

Rent bikes or a moped near Old Harbor; taxis meet the ferries and planes.
