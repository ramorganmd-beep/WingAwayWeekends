St. Petersburg

Land on the waterfront and walk downtown.

Albert Whitted Airport sits on the edge of downtown St. Petersburg, right on Tampa Bay. Park the airplane and walk to the Dalí Museum, the St. Pete Pier and the waterfront parks; stay downtown or out at the pink Don CeSar on St. Pete Beach, and have smoked fish at Ted Peters on the way.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Albert Whitted, downtown on the bay.

KSPG, Albert Whitted, is a towered downtown airport with a 3,676 ft runway (07/25) on the shore of Tampa Bay. Sheltair is the FBO. St. Pete–Clearwater International (KPIE) is the long-runway alternative north of town.

The FBO

Sheltair SPG

540 First Street SE, St. Petersburg, FL · reported hours 6 a.m.–9 p.m.
