Thousand Islands

Castles and wooden boats on the St. Lawrence.

The Thousand Islands are a scatter of nearly 1,900 islands in the St. Lawrence River, where Gilded Age millionaires built summer castles. Land at Watertown International, stay at the 1000 Islands Harbor Hotel in Clayton, see one of the best wooden-boat collections in the world at the Antique Boat Museum, take a boat to Boldt Castle and Singer Castle, and have a beer at Wood Boat Brewery.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Watertown.

KART, Watertown International, is a non-towered county airport at 331 feet with runways of 7,001 and 4,999 feet, about 25 minutes from Clayton.

The FBO

Watertown International Airport (Jefferson County)

Watertown International Airport, Dexter, NY

315-786-6001

The county runs the FBO. Fort Drum’s restricted airspace is just east of the field; brief it carefully. The Canadian border runs down the river; do not cross it without the required filings. No live fuel price is shown.

Upstate New York flying: lake-effect snow and low ceilings from November to March, summer thunderstorms, morning valley fog, and icing in the cold months. About 630 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Car and boats.

Rent a car at the airport; boat tours leave from Clayton and Alexandria Bay.
