Marfa & Big Bend

High-desert art, dark skies and the Marfa Lights.

Marfa is a tiny ranching town in the high desert of West Texas that became a world-famous art destination after the artist Donald Judd moved here in the 1970s. See his work at the Chinati Foundation, watch for the mysterious Marfa Lights, join a star party at McDonald Observatory under some of the darkest skies in the country, and drive to the canyons of Big Bend.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in the high desert.

KMRF, Marfa Municipal, is a few minutes north of town, at about 4,850 feet.

The FBO

Marfa Municipal Airport (Presidio County)

45101 State Highway 17, Marfa, TX 79843

432-295-0979

Self-serve 100LL and Jet A are listed around the clock; confirm the FBO phone (sources differ), hours and a rental car ahead, since rentals are scarce. No live fuel price is shown.

High elevation and summer heat make density altitude a serious factor; check performance. Winds can be strong, and the area is remote. This is the longest trip on the site, about 1,290 nm: plan two fuel stops and four or more nights.

Getting around

You’ll need a car.

Marfa is walkable, but Fort Davis, the observatory and Big Bend are an hour or more away.
