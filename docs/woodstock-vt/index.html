Woodstock

A storybook Vermont village with a covered bridge and a grand inn.

Woodstock is the Vermont village of postcards, with a green, a covered bridge and Federal houses. Stay at the Woodstock Inn, visit the Jersey cows at Billings Farm, walk the carriage roads of Marsh-Billings-Rockefeller National Historical Park, look into Quechee Gorge and watch glassblowers at Simon Pearce, then dine by the waterfall.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Lebanon.

KLEB, Lebanon Municipal Airport in New Hampshire, is a towered field about 25 minutes from Woodstock.
