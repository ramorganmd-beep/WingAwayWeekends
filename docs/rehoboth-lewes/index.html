Rehoboth Beach & Lewes

A lively beach town and a historic harbor village.

Rehoboth Beach and Lewes are a perfect pair. Walk Rehoboth’s boardwalk and eat at its creative restaurants, then slow down in Lewes, a 1631 harbor village with a beer-lover’s inn from Dogfish Head. Bike the trails and dunes at Cape Henlopen State Park, and take the ferry across the bay to Cape May for a day.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Georgetown.

KGED, Delaware Coastal Airport, is a non-towered field with a 5,500-foot runway, about 20 minutes from Lewes and Rehoboth.

The FBO

Mid-Atlantic coastal weather: summer haze and afternoon thunderstorms, sea fog in spring, and busy airspace near Philadelphia, Baltimore and Dover AFB. About 350 nm from Myrtle Beach.

Getting around

Rent a car.

The towns are walkable; bikes on the trails; a car between towns.
