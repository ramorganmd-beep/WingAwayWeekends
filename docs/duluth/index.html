Duluth & the North Shore

Lake Superior, ore ships and a lighthouse on the cliffs.

Duluth climbs a hillside above Lake Superior, the largest freshwater lake in the world. Watch 1,000-foot ore ships pass under the Aerial Lift Bridge, walk the Lakewalk, tour a lakefront mansion, then drive the North Shore to waterfalls and Split Rock Lighthouse on its cliff.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land above the city.

KDLH, Duluth International, is a towered airport about 15 minutes from Canal Park.

The FBO

Vantage Aviation · Duluth

4535 Airport Approach Road, Duluth, MN 55811

218-727-2911

Some directories list the FBO as Monaco Air Duluth; the airport site names Vantage Aviation. Confirm the name, hours and fees, and ask about rental cars. No live fuel price is shown.

This is a long trip, about 1,000 nm from Myrtle Beach, so plan one or two fuel stops and three or four nights. Lake Superior brings fog, low clouds and early winter. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

You’ll want a car.

Canal Park and the Lakewalk are walkable; the North Shore drive needs a car.
