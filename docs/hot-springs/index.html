Hot Springs

Thermal baths on Bathhouse Row.

Hot Springs grew up around 47 natural hot springs in the Ouachita Mountains, and the grand bathhouses of the 1910s and ’20s now form the heart of a national park. Soak in thermal water at a historic bathhouse, walk Bathhouse Row, drink a beer brewed with spring water, and stroll the gardens on Lake Hamilton.
