San Diego & Coronado

Carriers, beaches and the Hotel del.

San Diego is a Navy city with near-perfect weather, wide beaches and a fleet of aircraft carriers across the bay at North Island. Land at Montgomery-Gibbs Executive, stay at the Hotel del Coronado on the beach or the Lodge at Torrey Pines above the ocean, walk the flight deck of the USS Midway, spend a day in Balboa Park and at the zoo, and watch the sea lions at La Jolla Cove.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Montgomery-Gibbs.

KMYF, Montgomery-Gibbs Executive, is a towered general-aviation field at 427 feet with a 4,598-foot main runway, about 15 minutes from downtown and 25 from Coronado.

The FBO

CrownAir Aviation

Montgomery-Gibbs Executive Airport, San Diego, CA

858-292-1181

The field lies under the San Diego Class B; Miramar, North Island and Lindbergh are close by. Brief the VFR corridors carefully. No live fuel price is shown.

Southern California coast: marine layer and low stratus most mornings from May to July (“May Gray” and “June Gloom”), usually burning off by midday; Santa Ana winds and turbulence in fall and winter; busy, complex airspace. About 1910 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Car or rideshare.

A car or rideshare connects the beaches, the bay and the park; Coronado is walkable once you’re there.
