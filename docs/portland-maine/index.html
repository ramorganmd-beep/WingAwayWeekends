Portland, Maine

Lobster rolls, the Old Port and lighthouses.

Portland is one of the country’s great small food cities. Walk the cobblestones of the Old Port, eat oysters at Eventide and fries at Duckfat, dine at Fore Street, ride a ferry into Casco Bay and drive out to Portland Head Light, one of America’s most photographed lighthouses.
