Harris Ranch

Land, tie down, walk to a steak dinner.

Harris Ranch Resort sits beside Interstate 5 halfway between Los Angeles and San Francisco, with its own paved, lighted airstrip on the south side of the property. Tie down for free, walk to your room, swim in the heated pool, have a Prime steak dinner and a slice of pie from the bakery, and fly on after breakfast. It is the classic overnight stop on a long West Coast trip, not a full weekend.

01 / Aviation & getting around

The resort’s own airstrip.

3O8 is a public-use, untowered airstrip owned by the resort: one runway, 14/32, 2,820 ft long and only 30 ft wide. Parking and tie-downs are free, and Shell self-serve 100LL is available 24 hours.

Checked Nov 2026. Crew cars and pilot perks change often: call the FBO before you go. How to have a rental car waiting at the FBO · Know something we missed? Tell us.

There is no FBO staff: for questions about landing, the resort asks pilots to call 559-935-0717, extension 6444.

The runway is narrow (30 ft): plan a stable approach and check crosswinds. A power line 40 ft above the ground runs 0.3 nm south of the airport, and run-ups are not allowed at the approach end of runway 32. No instrument procedures; New Coalinga Municipal (C80) is 6 nm southwest.

Summer afternoons in the San Joaquin Valley are hot: check density altitude and your takeoff numbers. Winter tule fog can sit in the valley for days.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Narrow runway

30 ft wide and 2,820 ft long, with a power line just south: brief it before you go.

Steakhouse days

Prime Steakhouse is closed Tuesday and Wednesday; the Ranch Kitchen and lounge are open daily.

Fuel

Self-serve 100LL, 24 hours. No jet fuel listed and no FBO staff.

Plan B

In tule fog or summer heat, New Coalinga Municipal (C80) is 6 nm away.
