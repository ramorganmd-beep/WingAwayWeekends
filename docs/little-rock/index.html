Little Rock

A river city of history, bridges and trails.

Little Rock is an easy, friendly capital on the Arkansas River. Tour Central High School, where nine Black students integrated the school in 1957, visit the Clinton Presidential Center, bike the Arkansas River Trail over the Big Dam Bridge, climb Pinnacle Mountain for the view and stay at the elegant 1870s Capital Hotel.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Clinton National.

KLIT, Bill and Hillary Clinton National Airport, is a towered airport about 10 minutes from downtown and the River Market.

The FBO

Atlantic Aviation

Clinton National Airport, Little Rock, AR

501-376-7777

Signature Aviation (501-374-6582) is a second FBO. Confirm hours, fees and rental cars. No live fuel price is shown.

Summer brings afternoon storms. About 665 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Rent a car.

Downtown and the River Market are walkable; a car or bikes cover the trail, the bridge and Pinnacle Mountain.
