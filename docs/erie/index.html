Erie & Presque Isle

Beaches, sunsets and wine country on Lake Erie.

Erie has miles of sandy beaches on the Presque Isle peninsula, sunsets over the lake and a wine country of vineyards along the shore. Bike the peninsula’s loop past lighthouses, tour the tall ship Flagship Niagara, have dinner on a rooftop over the bay and spend an afternoon tasting wine.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Erie.

KERI, Erie International, is a towered airport about 10 minutes from the bayfront and Presque Isle.

The FBO

North Coast Air

Erie International Airport, Erie, PA

814-836-9220

Great Lakes weather: lake-effect clouds and snow from late fall through winter, summer thunderstorms and fog over cold water in spring. About 510 nm from Myrtle Beach. A long nonstop leg or a fuel stop.
