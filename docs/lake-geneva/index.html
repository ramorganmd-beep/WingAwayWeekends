Lake Geneva

Gilded Age mansions on a clear spring-fed lake.

Lake Geneva has been Chicago’s summer retreat since the 1870s, and grand estates still line its shore. Walk part of the 21-mile Shore Path past the mansions, ride the mail boat as carriers leap to the piers, tour Black Point Estate, dine at the lakefront Baker House and unwind at a resort spa.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Burlington.

KBUU, Burlington Municipal, is a non-towered field about 20 minutes from Lake Geneva.

The FBO

Aero 290

Burlington Municipal Airport, Burlington, WI

262-342-5003

Great Lakes weather: lake-effect clouds and snow from late fall through winter, summer thunderstorms and fog over cold water in spring. Chicago airspace lies to the south. About 700 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Rent a car.

Downtown is walkable; a car covers the resorts and the airport.
