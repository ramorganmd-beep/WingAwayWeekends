Huntsville

Rocket City: a Saturn V, a mountain and a lively downtown.

Huntsville built the rockets that took Americans to the Moon, and the U.S. Space & Rocket Center has a full Saturn V hanging overhead. Add a walk at the Huntsville Botanical Garden, sunset from Monte Sano Mountain, studios at Lowe Mill and dinner downtown, and it is an easy, aviation-minded weekend.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Fly to Rocket City.

KHSV, Huntsville International, is a towered airport about 15 minutes from the Space & Rocket Center and downtown.

The FBO

Signature Aviation

Huntsville International Airport, Huntsville, AL

256-772-9341

Huntsville Executive Airport (KMDQ), north of the city, is a non-towered alternative with its own FBO (256-828-1403). Confirm hours, fees and cars. No live fuel price is shown.

About 395 nm from Myrtle Beach. Expect mountain-adjacent weather and summer afternoon storms.

Getting around

Rent a car.

Downtown is walkable, but the space center, garden and Monte Sano need a car.
