Shenandoah & Luray

Skyline Drive, caverns and a mountain lodge.

Luray sits in the Page Valley between the Blue Ridge and Massanutten Mountain, a few minutes from Shenandoah National Park. Land in the valley, explore the great caverns, drive Skyline Drive to overlooks above the clouds, and stay at a lodge on the ridge or a gracious inn in town.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in the valley below the park.

KLUA, Luray Caverns Airport, is a few minutes from Luray and the caverns, and about 15 minutes from the park’s Thornton Gap entrance on Skyline Drive.

The FBO

Luray Caverns Airport

1504 Airport Road, Luray, VA 22835

540-743-6070

A small field with listed hours of about 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Rental cars are limited here; call ahead about a courtesy car or a rental delivered to the airport. Ask about overnight parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

This is mountain flying: the Blue Ridge rises just east of the field and Massanutten Mountain to the west. Expect turbulence on windy days and valley fog in the morning. The Washington, DC SFRA lies to the east; review terrain, the sectional and current NOTAMs and TFRs.

Getting around

You’ll need a car.

Skyline Drive, the lodges and the trailheads are spread along the ridge. The speed limit on Skyline Drive is 35 mph, so allow plenty of time.

04 / Things to do

Underground and on top.

Mix one big view with one short hike each day.

01

Luray Caverns

The largest caverns in the East, with huge chambers and the Great Stalacpipe Organ. The ticket includes a car and carriage museum. About an hour underground.

Tickets and hours
02

Skyline Drive

A 105-mile road along the crest of the Blue Ridge with more than 70 overlooks. Thornton Gap to Big Meadows makes a beautiful half day.

Park information
03

Stony Man

A short, moderate walk near Skyland to cliffs with one of the best views in the park. Under two miles round trip.

04

Hawksbill & Dark Hollow Falls

Hawksbill is the park’s highest peak; Dark Hollow Falls is a short, steep walk to a waterfall near Big Meadows.

05

The Shenandoah River

Canoe, kayak or float the South Fork near Luray in summer with a local outfitter.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

A car first, then lodging and dinners.

Park entry

Shenandoah charges an entrance fee. Old Rag requires a separate day-use ticket in season.

Mountain flying

Ridges on both sides, morning fog and afternoon storms. Keep a backup day.

Seasons

Park lodges and Skyline Drive services close in winter, and the drive can close for snow or ice.
