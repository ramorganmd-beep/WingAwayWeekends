Kohler

A five-star resort village with a famous spa and golf.

Kohler is a planned village built around the plumbing company, and its resort is one of the Midwest’s finest. Stay at The American Club, spend an afternoon in the Kohler Waters Spa, play or tour Whistling Straits on the Lake Michigan bluffs and visit the John Michael Kohler Arts Center in Sheboygan.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Sheboygan.

KSBM, Sheboygan County Memorial, is a non-towered field about 5 minutes from Kohler.

The FBO

Great Lakes weather: lake-effect clouds and snow from late fall through winter, summer thunderstorms and fog over cold water in spring. About 740 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.
