New Orleans

Jazz, Creole dining and the French Quarter, from a 1934 lakefront airport.

New Orleans may be the best food-and-music weekend in the country, and Lakefront Airport puts you about 15 minutes from the French Quarter. Hear traditional jazz at Preservation Hall, linger over a jazz brunch at Commander’s Palace, ride the St. Charles streetcar through the Garden District and spend a morning at the National WWII Museum.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on the lakefront.

KNEW, New Orleans Lakefront Airport, is a towered field on a peninsula in Lake Pontchartrain, with an art deco terminal from 1934. It is much closer to the French Quarter than MSY.

The FBO

Flightline First

New Orleans Lakefront Airport, New Orleans, LA 70126

504-244-4448

Skyborne (504-946-3835) is a second FBO on the field. Confirm hours, ramp and overnight fees and courtesy-car availability. No live fuel price is shown.

New Orleans Class B airspace surrounds the field; brief the arrival and the lake procedures. Summer brings afternoon storms, and hurricane season runs June to November. About 610 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Leave the car behind.

The French Quarter is best on foot; the St. Charles streetcar reaches the Garden District, and a rideshare from the airport takes about 15 minutes.
