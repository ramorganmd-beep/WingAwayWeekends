Palm Beach

Gilded Florida: Worth Avenue, The Breakers and Flagler’s mansion.

Palm Beach is Florida at its most glamorous: an island of grand hotels, Mediterranean mansions and hedge-lined streets. Stay at The Breakers, shop and stroll Worth Avenue’s courtyards, tour Henry Flagler’s Gilded Age mansion, and bike the Lake Trail along the Intracoastal at sunset.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Lantana, 20 minutes south.

KLNA, Palm Beach County Park Airport in Lantana, is a general aviation airport about 20 minutes from the island.

The FBO

Stellar Aviation of South Palm Beach

2633 Lantana Road, Lantana, FL 33462

561-656-9815

Listed hours are about 8 a.m. to 6:30 p.m. Ask about rental cars, parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Presidential and VIP TFRs over Palm Beach are common and can close nearby airspace with little notice; check TFRs before every flight. Palm Beach International’s Class C airspace is just north. Summer brings afternoon storms.

Getting around

A car, then bikes.

Rent a car to reach the island; then bikes or walking for Worth Avenue and the Lake Trail.
