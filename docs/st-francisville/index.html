St. Francisville

Oak-shaded gardens and historic inns in Louisiana’s plantation country.

St. Francisville is a small, beautifully kept town on the bluffs above the Mississippi, about 45 minutes north of Baton Rouge. Stay at a historic inn, tour the gardens at Rosedown, visit the house where John James Audubon painted birds, hike to waterfalls in the Tunica Hills and eat very well for such a small place.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in Baton Rouge.

KBTR, Baton Rouge Metropolitan Airport, is a towered airport with rental cars, about 45 minutes south of St. Francisville.

The FBO

Velocity BTR

Baton Rouge Metropolitan Airport, Baton Rouge, LA

225-355-9052

Williams Jet Center (225-235-3661) is a second FBO. Confirm hours, fees and rental-car pickup. No live fuel price is shown.

False River Regional (KHZR) is closer but has limited ground transportation. Summer brings afternoon storms. About 650 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.

Getting around

Rent a car at BTR.

You need a car for the drive north and for the historic sites; the town itself is walkable.
