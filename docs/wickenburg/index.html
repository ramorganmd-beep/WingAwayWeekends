Wickenburg Dude Ranches

Saddle up in the Sonoran Desert.

Wickenburg is an old gold-mining town that became famous for its guest ranches, where you can ride through saguaros by day and eat by a campfire at night. Land at Wickenburg Municipal, stay at Rancho de los Caballeros, the Flying E or Kay El Bar, ride out into the desert, and spend an afternoon at the Sigler Western Museum, one of the best collections of Western art in the country.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Wickenburg.

E25, Wickenburg Municipal, is a non-towered town airport at 2,379 feet with a 6,101-foot runway, a few minutes from town and the ranches.

The FBO

Town of Wickenburg (airport FBO)

Wickenburg Municipal Airport, Wickenburg, AZ

928-684-0754

Master Aircraft Services is also on the field (same number). Ask your ranch about airport pickup. Summer heat is extreme. No live fuel price is shown.

Desert Southwest flying: summer temperatures above 110°F with high density altitude, strong afternoon winds and turbulence, monsoon storms and dust from July to September, and long distances between airports. About 1680 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.
