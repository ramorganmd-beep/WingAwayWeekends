Hudson Valley

River estates, great food and vintage airplanes.

Two hours north of Manhattan, the Hudson Valley is a land of river views, grand estates and small towns full of good food. Tour FDR’s home, walk a bridge 212 feet above the Hudson, dine at the Culinary Institute of America and watch World War I biplanes fly at the Old Rhinebeck Aerodrome.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in the middle of the valley.

KPOU, Hudson Valley Regional, is a towered airport south of Poughkeepsie, about 25 minutes from Hyde Park and 35 from Rhinebeck.

The FBO

FlightLevel Dutchess

263 New Hackensack Road, Wappingers Falls, NY 12590

845-849-3770

Directories list hours of 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily. Ask about rental cars, parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

New York Class B airspace lies to the south; Stewart’s Class C is across the river. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs, and plan a fuel stop for most piston aircraft.

Getting around

You’ll need a car.

04 / Things to do

Estates, art and old airplanes.

Choose one big outing a day.

01

Old Rhinebeck Aerodrome

A living museum of pioneer and World War I aircraft, with weekend air shows in summer and fall, and biplane rides. A must for pilots.

Shows and hours
02

Home of Franklin D. Roosevelt

FDR’s home, library and museum in Hyde Park, next to the Vanderbilt Mansion.

Park information
03

Walkway Over the Hudson

A 1.3-mile walk across an old railroad bridge 212 feet above the river.

Walkway information
04

Storm King Art Center

Huge outdoor sculptures across 500 acres of fields and hills, about 40 minutes south.

Tickets
05

Dia Beacon

A major museum of contemporary art in a former box factory in Beacon.
