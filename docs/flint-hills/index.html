Flint Hills

The last great tallgrass prairie.

The Flint Hills hold most of North America’s remaining tallgrass prairie, rolling and endless under a huge sky. Stay at the Grand Central Hotel in Cottonwood Falls, hike or take a bus tour among the bison at Tallgrass Prairie National Preserve, drive the Flint Hills Scenic Byway and have dinner at the 1857 Hays House in Council Grove.
