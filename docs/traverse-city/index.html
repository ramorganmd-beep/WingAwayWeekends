Traverse City

Dunes, wineries and blue water on Grand Traverse Bay.

Traverse City sits at the foot of Grand Traverse Bay, surrounded by cherry orchards and two peninsulas of vineyards. Climb the dunes at Sleeping Bear, taste wine on the Old Mission and Leelanau peninsulas, stay at a winery inn above the bay and eat farm-to-table dinners in town.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Cherry Capital.

KTVC, Cherry Capital Airport, is a towered field on the edge of town, about 10 minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Great Lakes weather: lake-effect clouds and snow from late fall through winter, summer thunderstorms and fog over cold water in spring. About 730 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.
