Lincoln

A landmark capitol, the Haymarket and a quilt museum.

Lincoln is an easygoing capital and college town. Climb to the observation deck of the Nebraska State Capitol, a 1930s landmark, wander the brick Haymarket district, see American art at the Sheldon and remarkable quilts at the International Quilt Museum, then have dinner at Dish.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Lincoln.

KLNK, Lincoln Airport, is a towered airport about 10 minutes from downtown. Duncan Aviation is headquartered here.

The FBO

Duncan Aviation

Lincoln Airport, Lincoln, NE

402-475-2611

Atlantic Aviation is a second FBO. Confirm hours, fees and cars. No live fuel price is shown.

Plains weather: strong winds, spring and summer thunderstorms (this is tornado country) and fast-moving winter systems. About 950 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.
