Jackson Hole & Grand Teton

Land inside Grand Teton National Park.

Jackson Hole Airport sits on the sagebrush floor of Grand Teton National Park with the Tetons rising straight up to the west. The town of Jackson is ten minutes south, with board sidewalks and elk-antler arches. Spend a day in the park, ride the tram at Teton Village, float the Snake River, look for bison and moose, and drive north to Yellowstone if you have the time.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A mountain airport inside a national park.

KJAC, Jackson Hole, is a towered field at 6,451 feet with a 6,300-foot runway. Strict noise-abatement procedures apply. Expect high density altitude in summer and winter snow and wind.

The FBO

Jackson Hole Flight Services (airport FBO)

Jackson Hole Airport, Jackson, WY

307-733-4767

Prior permission may be required for overnight parking; call ahead. Read the airport’s noise-abatement procedures. No live fuel price is shown.

Mountain flying: high density altitude on summer afternoons, mountain wave and strong winds over the ridges, afternoon thunderstorms from July to September, and winter snow and icing. Consider a mountain-flying course before you go. About 1590 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.
