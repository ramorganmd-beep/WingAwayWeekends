Rangeley Lakes

A mountain lake town with a seaplane base in the harbor.

Rangeley sits on its namesake lake among the western Maine mountains, a sporting town where floatplanes have long ferried anglers to remote camps. Fly in to the lake or the town airport, stay at the historic Rangeley Inn or a lakeside lodge, take a scenic flight with Acadian Seaplanes, drive to the Height of Land overlook and paddle at sunset.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on the lake or at the town airport.

8B0, Steven A. Bean Municipal, is a non-towered field with a 4,300-foot runway about 2 miles from town. Floatplanes can use M57, Rangeley Lake, a public-use seaplane base.

The FBO

Acadian Seaplanes

Steven A. Bean Municipal Airport, Rangeley, ME

207-864-5307

Mountain airport: check density altitude and winds. Confirm fuel and a car. No live fuel price is shown.

Maine mountain and lake weather: fog over the lakes in the morning, fast-changing summer storms, gusty winds funneling down the lakes, and ice from about mid-November to mid-May. About 780 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.
