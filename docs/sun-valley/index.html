Sun Valley & Ketchum

A classic ski town in the Idaho mountains.

Sun Valley opened in 1936 as the country’s first destination ski resort, and Ketchum next door still has a small-town Western feel. Fly into Friedman Memorial in Hailey, about 12 miles south, stay at the Sun Valley Lodge or the Limelight, ski or hike Bald Mountain, raise a glass at the Pioneer Saloon and drive north to the Sawtooths.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Hailey in a narrow valley.

KSUN, Friedman Memorial in Hailey, is a towered field at 5,320 feet with a 7,550-foot runway in a narrow canyon. Arrivals and departures normally use opposite directions; read the airport’s procedures before you go.

The FBO

Atlantic Aviation Sun Valley

Friedman Memorial Airport, Hailey, ID

208-788-9511

Terrain on three sides; departures often go out the way you came in. Compute performance carefully. No live fuel price is shown.

Mountain flying: high density altitude on summer afternoons, mountain wave and strong winds over the ridges, afternoon thunderstorms from July to September, and winter snow and icing. Consider a mountain-flying course before you go. About 1750 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Shuttle or car.

Ketchum and Sun Valley are walkable and linked by free buses; a car helps for the Sawtooths.
