Kiawah Island

Golf, beach and the Sanctuary.

Kiawah Island is a quiet barrier island south of Charleston, with ten miles of wide beach, maritime forest, marsh and five golf courses, including the Ocean Course, host of the PGA Championship. Land at Charleston Executive on Johns Island, stay at the oceanfront Sanctuary or a resort villa, bike the island’s trails, play golf, walk the beach to watch dolphins, see the Angel Oak, and spend an evening in Charleston.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on Johns Island.

KJZI, Charleston Executive, is a non-towered airport at 19 feet with runways of 5,350 and 4,313 feet on Johns Island, about 25 minutes from Kiawah.

The FBO

Atlantic Aviation

Charleston Executive Airport, Johns Island, SC

843-559-2401

Interstate Turbine is also on the field. Close to Charleston’s Class C airspace and military traffic from Joint Base Charleston. Ask the resort about transportation. No live fuel price is shown.

Carolina coast flying: summer afternoon thunderstorms, sea fog and low morning stratus, gusty sea breezes, and hurricanes from June to November. About 80 nm from Myrtle Beach.

Getting around

Shuttle or car.

Ask the resort about transportation; bikes are the best way around the island.
