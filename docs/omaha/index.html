Omaha

The Old Market, a famous zoo and a Cold War air museum.

Omaha is a friendly river city with a cobblestone warehouse district full of restaurants and galleries. Wander the Old Market, walk the pedestrian bridge across the Missouri, visit the renovated Joslyn Art Museum and Lauritzen Gardens, spend a morning at the Henry Doorly Zoo and see the bombers at the SAC Aerospace Museum.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Millard.

KMLE, Millard Airport, is a non-towered GA field in southwest Omaha, about 20 minutes from the Old Market.

The FBO

Oracle Aviation

Millard Airport, Omaha, NE

402-934-5300

Plains weather: strong winds, spring and summer thunderstorms (this is tornado country) and fast-moving winter systems. About 930 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.

Getting around

Rent a car.

The Old Market is walkable; a car covers the zoo, gardens and the SAC museum.
