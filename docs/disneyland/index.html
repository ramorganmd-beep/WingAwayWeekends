Anaheim & Disneyland

The original Disney park, from a small airport next door.

Fullerton Municipal is a small towered airport in north Orange County, a short drive from the Disneyland Resort in Anaheim, and much quieter than John Wayne or Long Beach. Stay at a Disney hotel or close by, spend a day at Disneyland Park or Disney California Adventure, and add Knott’s Berry Farm, a few minutes from the airport, for a second park day. The sweet spot is about 4 to 12.

Flying with children

Best for kids

Best ages: 4 to 12. The original Disney park, with a second park across the plaza and Knott’s Berry Farm a few minutes from the airport.

Top three for kids

  1. A day at Disneyland Park, or Pixar Pier and Avengers Campus at Disney California Adventure.
  2. Knott’s Berry Farm, from gentle kids’ rides to big coasters for teens.
  3. Discovery Cube Orange County, a hands-on science museum.

At the airport: KFUL is towered with a 3,121 ft runway under busy Los Angeles-area airspace. The FBO closes at 5 p.m.; check your performance numbers on warm days.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A small airport close to the parks.

KFUL, Fullerton Municipal, has a control tower and one runway, 6/24, 3,121 ft by 75 ft. General Aviation Co. is the FBO; it advertises no landing or ramp fees, and Hertz and Enterprise rental cars.

FBO hours are listed as 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. weekdays and 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. weekends: arrange late arrivals ahead. Confirm fees, parking and rental cars. No live fuel price is shown.

The runway is 3,121 ft: check your takeoff and landing numbers, especially on warm days. KFUL sits under busy Los Angeles-area airspace, with the Los Angeles Class B and other towered airports close by; brief the arrival before you go.

A morning marine layer is common in late spring and early summer; it usually burns off by late morning.

Getting around

Rental car or Uber.

The Disneyland Resort is a short drive from the airport, and Knott’s Berry Farm in Buena Park is even closer. Reserve a rental car with child seats through the FBO, or use Uber with your own car seats.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Best ages

Disneyland Park suits children about 3 to 10; older kids and teens may prefer California Adventure or Knott’s bigger coasters.

Book in this order

Park tickets and any reservations first, then the hotel, then the rental car with child seats.

Short runway

KFUL’s runway is 3,121 ft. Load and plan with your aircraft’s numbers; on a hot day, consider a lighter fuel load.

Car seats

Bring your own or reserve them with the rental car; Uber drivers do not supply them.
