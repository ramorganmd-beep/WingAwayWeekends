San Luis Obispo & Pismo Beach

A college town, the beach and wine country, ten minutes apart.

San Luis Obispo County Regional sits just south of town, with a downtown full of restaurants along San Luis Creek and Pismo Beach about 15 minutes away. Stay at the famously over-the-top Madonna Inn or on the bluffs in Pismo, walk the Thursday night farmers’ market, taste in the Edna Valley and end with chowder by the pier. For the beach itself, Oceano County Airport (L52) is half a mile from the sand, with a fly-in campground beside the ramp.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A towered field, or the beach strip.

KSBP has a control tower and two runways: 11/29, 6,101 ft, and 7/25, 2,500 ft. ACI Jet is the FBO. Landing fees apply only to aircraft of 6,000 lb and up, so most piston singles pay none.

Checked Nov 2026. Crew cars and pilot perks change often: call the FBO before you go. How to have a rental car waiting at the FBO · Know something we missed? Tell us.

Noise-sensitive airport; runway 29 is the calm-wind runway. Waterfowl gather on and near the field in winter. Transient parking over two hours: contact the FBO. Coastal fog and low clouds are common, especially late spring and summer mornings.

Beach option: Oceano County Airport (L52), 8 nm south, is untowered with a 2,325 ft by 50 ft runway, self-serve 100LL, $15-a-night tie-downs and a fly-in-only campground ($25 a night, first come, restrooms, no showers). It is extremely noise sensitive: departing runway 29, hold runway heading until crossing the shoreline. Watch for kites along the beach.

Getting around

Rental car or Uber.

Downtown San Luis Obispo is about 10 minutes from KSBP and Pismo Beach about 15. From Oceano, the beach is a short walk; Enterprise in Grover Beach offers pickup, and taxis and rideshare serve the area.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Fog

Marine layer is common on summer and spring mornings; plan late-morning arrivals and departures.

Landing fees

KSBP charges general aviation only for aircraft of 6,000 lb and over.

Oceano option

L52 is short (2,325 ft) and narrow (50 ft), untowered and extremely noise sensitive; read the airport remarks before you go.

Book in this order

Hotel first, especially on summer and graduation weekends, then the rental car.
