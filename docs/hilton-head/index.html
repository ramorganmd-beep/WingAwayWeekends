Hilton Head

Land on the island. Ride a bike to the beach.

Hilton Head’s airport sits right on the island, minutes from wide, hard-packed beaches and more than 60 miles of bike paths under the live oaks. Ride to the beach, watch the sunset at Harbour Town’s red-and-white lighthouse, and book a long Lowcountry dinner on the water.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land on the island.

KHXD, Hilton Head Airport, is a towered field on the north end of the island, about 15–25 minutes from the beaches and Sea Pines.

The FBO

Signature Aviation

52 Gateway Circle, Hilton Head Island, SC 29926

843-689-3200

Signature lists courtesy and rental cars and can arrange resort transfers. Ask about overnight parking, facility fees and fee waivers with fuel. The airport has airline service and a relatively short runway; check performance for your aircraft, especially in summer heat. No live fuel price is shown.

Savannah’s Class C airspace lies just to the southwest. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs, and expect afternoon storms in summer.

Getting around

Bikes do most of the work.

Most resorts rent bikes, and the island’s paths and flat beaches make them the best way around. Use a rental car or rideshare for dinners across the island and the drive to Sea Pines.

04 / Things to do

Beach, bikes and the lighthouse.

Slow and easy. Leave plenty of time for the beach.

01

Bike the beach and the paths

At low tide the beach is firm enough to ride for miles. More than 60 miles of paved paths run through the island’s neighborhoods and forests.

02

Harbour Town & the lighthouse

The red-and-white lighthouse in Sea Pines overlooks the yacht basin and the 18th green of Harbour Town Golf Links. Climb it for the view, then stay for sunset.

03

Golf

Harbour Town Golf Links, home of the RBC Heritage, is the headline, with dozens of other courses on and near the island.

04

Sea Pines Forest Preserve

Boardwalks and trails through marsh, ponds and live oaks, a quiet hour away from the beach.

05

Dolphins and Daufuskie Island

Dolphin cruises and kayak tours leave from several marinas, and ferries run to car-free Daufuskie Island for lunch and a golf-cart ramble.

April

RBC Heritage

The island’s PGA Tour event at Harbour Town. Exciting, and the busiest week of spring; book early or avoid it.

Spring & fall

Best seasons

Warm days, quieter beaches and easier tee times. Summer is hot and busy, with afternoon storms.

Live jazz

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

Lodging first, then dinners, tee times and any spa time.

Heritage week

The RBC Heritage in April fills the island. Check dates before booking.

Runway & heat

KHXD’s runway is on the short side for some aircraft. Check performance, especially on hot days.

Hurricane season

June through November. Watch the tropics and keep plans flexible.
