Bar Harbor & Acadia

Granite cliffs, lobster and the first sunrise.

On Mount Desert Island, Acadia National Park’s granite mountains rise straight out of the Atlantic. Fly in over the islands, watch the sunrise from Cadillac Mountain, bike the old carriage roads, have popovers at Jordan Pond and a lobster dinner on the dock.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at the gateway to the island.

KBHB, Hancock County–Bar Harbor, sits on the mainland in Trenton, about 20 minutes from downtown Bar Harbor.

The FBO

Modern Aviation · Bar Harbor

112 Caruso Drive, Trenton, ME 04605

207-667-5534FBO details

Ask the FBO about rental cars, overnight parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

This is a long trip, about 800 nm from Myrtle Beach; you may need a fuel stop depending on your aircraft, and it’s worth considering a three-night weekend. Coastal fog is frequent in summer. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

A car, then the park shuttle.

Rent a car for the weekend. In season, the free Island Explorer buses link Bar Harbor with the park’s trailheads and beaches.

04 / Things to do

Mountains that meet the sea.

Start early. The park is busiest from late morning.

01

Cadillac Mountain

The highest point on the Atlantic coast of the U.S., and one of the first places in the country to see the sunrise. Vehicle reservations are required in season.

Park information
02

Ocean Path & Thunder Hole

An easy walk along the granite shore from Sand Beach to Otter Cliff.

03

The carriage roads

Forty-five miles of crushed-stone roads and stone bridges, closed to cars. Rent bikes in Bar Harbor.

04

Jordan Pond Path

A flat loop around a clear mountain lake below the Bubbles.

05

Bar Island at low tide

Walk across a gravel bar from town to Bar Island when the tide is out. Check the tide table.

07 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

Lodging and a car first, then the Cadillac reservation and dinners.

Long flight

About 800 nm. Check whether your aircraft needs a fuel stop, and consider a third night.

Seasons

Many restaurants and the Island Explorer run late spring to mid-October.

Coastal fog

Summer fog is common. Keep a backup day.
