Virginia Hunt Country

Stone walls, horse farms and serious wine.

West of Washington, the rolling Piedmont around Middleburg is fox-hunting and steeplechase country, with more than 50 wineries in Loudoun County alone. Stay at a historic inn or a horse-country resort, taste some of Virginia’s best wines, and save an hour for the Space Shuttle at the Udvar-Hazy Center.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Two good doors to horse country.

KJYO, Leesburg Executive, is about 25 minutes from Middleburg. It sits inside the Washington, DC SFRA, so many pilots will prefer KHWY, Warrenton-Fauquier, just outside it.

The FBOs

Kuhn Jet Center · KJYO

1001 Sycolin Road, Leesburg, VA 20175

703-665-3000
Warrenton-Fauquier Airport FBO · KHWY

1533 Iris Trail, Midland, VA 22728

540-422-8285

Washington, DC SFRA: KJYO lies inside the Special Flight Rules Area. You need the FAA’s SFRA training, a filed SFRA flight plan, a discrete transponder code and two-way radio contact, and you must follow the published Leesburg procedures. If that is not for you, fly to KHWY (about 300 NM, ~2 hr 15 min at 150 kt), outside the SFRA and about 25 minutes from Middleburg.

Kuhn Jet Center lists free courtesy cars for local use and fueling from early morning into the evening. Warrenton-Fauquier lists a courtesy car and daytime hours (about 8–5), with a fee for after-hours service. Ask both about two-night parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Getting around

Book a driver for tasting day.

Wineries are spread across back roads, 15–30 minutes apart. Rent a car for the weekend (courtesy cars are for short local trips), and hire a private wine-tour driver for the tasting day.

Pilot note / Wine & flying

Taste on the non-flying day.

FAA rules (14 CFR 91.17) require at least 8 hours from bottle to throttle, a blood alcohol below 0.04% and no lingering effects. Many pilots use 12–24 hours as a personal minimum, since a hangover can impair you long after the legal limit is met. Plan the tasting day for the middle of the weekend, keep the evening before departure dry, and let a driver or shuttle handle the roads. If the pilot wants a full tasting day, add a third night. Many Loudoun wineries limit group sizes, require reservations on weekends or are adults-only; check before you go.

05 / Things to do

Horse country, history and one great hangar.

Mix a slow village morning with a big aviation afternoon.

01

Steven F. Udvar-Hazy Center

The Smithsonian’s huge companion museum beside Dulles: the Space Shuttle Discovery, an SR-71 Blackbird, the Enola Gay and hundreds more aircraft. About 30 minutes from KJYO. Admission is free; parking is paid.

Museum information
02

Middleburg’s main street

Stone buildings, boutiques, tack shops, galleries and cafés in one of Virginia’s prettiest small towns.

03

National Sporting Library & Museum

Art and books on horses, hunting and the sporting life, in Middleburg. Small, beautiful and very hunt country.

Visit the museum
04

Historic Leesburg

Brick sidewalks, restaurants and shops in a colonial-era town center, near KJYO. Morven Park’s mansion and gardens are on the edge of town.

May & October

Virginia Gold Cup races

Steeplechase racing at Great Meadow in The Plains: the Virginia Gold Cup in spring and the International Gold Cup in fall. Tailgates, hats and big crowds; book far ahead.

September–October

Harvest and fall color

Harvest at the wineries and color in the hills. The most beautiful and busiest season.

December

Middleburg Christmas parade

Horses and hounds lead the town’s holiday parade. Charming, and the inns fill early.

Day trip version

Just for the day

  1. Arrive

    Land at KJYO (SFRA procedures) or KHWY mid-morning and pick up a car.

  2. Midday

    The Udvar-Hazy Center: the Space Shuttle, the SR-71 and more.

  3. Afternoon

    Lunch and a stroll in Middleburg, with a tasting for the non-pilot, then fly home.

FRI

Arrive & a big hangar

  1. Late-morning arrival

    Land at KJYO or KHWY, subject to weather, the SFRA and your actual flight plan, and pick up the car.

  2. Afternoon

    The Udvar-Hazy Center, then on to Middleburg to check in.

  3. Evening

    Dinner at The Red Fox Inn & Tavern.

SAT

Wine country day

  1. Late morning

    A private driver to Boxwood and Greenhill, with lunch between tastings.

  2. Afternoon

    A glass at Cana or 50 West, or a spa afternoon at Salamander.

  3. Evening

    Dinner at The Conservatory at Goodstone. Keep the night early if you fly tomorrow.

SUN

An easy departure

  1. Morning

    Breakfast and a walk along Middleburg’s main street, or the National Sporting Library.

  2. Before leaving

    Return the car, file the SFRA plan if departing KJYO, and allow time at the FBO.

  3. Fly home

    Only when the pilot is well clear of the 8-hour rule and feels fully fit to fly.

08 / Good to know

Keep the weekend easy to change.

These details make the difference between an appealing list and a usable getaway.

Book in this order

Lodging and a wine-tour driver first, then dinners and any spa time.

The SFRA

KJYO requires SFRA procedures. KHWY, outside it, is the simpler choice for many pilots.

Race weekends

The Gold Cup weekends in May and October fill every inn for miles. Check dates before booking.

Wine & flying
