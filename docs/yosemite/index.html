Yosemite

Granite, waterfalls and giant sequoias.

Yosemite Valley is one of the most famous views on Earth: El Capitan, Half Dome and Yosemite Falls rising thousands of feet above the meadows. Land at Mariposa-Yosemite in the Sierra foothills, about an hour and a half from the valley, stay at the Ahwahnee or a foothill lodge, walk to the base of the falls, stand at Glacier Point at sunset and walk among the sequoias of Mariposa Grove.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land in the Sierra foothills.

KMPI, Mariposa-Yosemite, is a non-towered county airport at 2,254 feet with a 3,305-foot runway. Self-serve fuel is available 24 hours with a credit card.

The FBO

Mariposa-Yosemite Airport (County of Mariposa)

Mariposa-Yosemite Airport, Mariposa, CA

209-966-5356

Airborrn Aviation Services (209-966-2143) is also on the field. Short runway with mountains to the north and terrain dropping away beside Runway 8; the chart supplement reports cracking and loose debris on the runway. Arrange a rental car in advance. No live fuel price is shown.

Inland Central California: hot summer afternoons with high density altitude, haze and wildfire smoke some summers, winter valley fog (tule fog) and Sierra storms. About 2000 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Car.

A car is essential. Yosemite Valley is about 1 hour 30 minutes from Mariposa; park entry reservations may be required in peak season.
