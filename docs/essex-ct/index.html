Essex & the Connecticut River

A 1776 inn, a steam train and river villages.

The lower Connecticut River is lined with quiet villages. Stay at the Griswold Inn, open since 1776, ride the Essex Steam Train and riverboat, tour Gillette Castle on its bluff, see a musical at the Goodspeed Opera House and visit the Florence Griswold Museum, home of the Old Lyme Impressionists.
