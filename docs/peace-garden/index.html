International Peace Garden & Turtle Mountains

A garden on the border and lakes in the woods.

The Turtle Mountains are a surprise: wooded hills and lakes rising from the prairie along the Canadian border. Walk the International Peace Garden, which straddles the border, rent a cabin at Lake Metigoshe State Park, paddle quiet lakes and watch for loons. Bring a passport if you plan to cross.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Bottineau.

D09, Bottineau Municipal, is a small non-towered field about 15 minutes from Lake Metigoshe and 25 from the Peace Garden.

High plains weather: strong winds, summer thunderstorms and fast-moving winter systems. Check density altitude in summer. About 1320 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.
