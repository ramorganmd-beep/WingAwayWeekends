Bretton Woods & Franconia Notch

A grand hotel beneath Mount Washington.

The Omni Mount Washington Resort is one of America’s last grand hotels, its red roof and white veranda set beneath the Presidential Range. Ride the Cog Railway to the summit of Mount Washington, walk the Flume Gorge in Franconia Notch, take the Cannon Mountain tram and eat pancakes at Polly’s.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Mount Washington Regional.

KHIE, Mount Washington Regional Airport in Whitefield, is a non-towered field about 25 minutes from Bretton Woods.

The FBO

Mount Washington Regional Airport

Mount Washington Regional Airport, Whitefield, NH

603-631-0621

Confirm FBO hours, fees and ground transportation. No live fuel price is shown.

Mountain and lake weather: watch ceilings, icing in the cold months and afternoon storms in summer. Mount Washington has some of the worst weather in the world. About 730 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights.
