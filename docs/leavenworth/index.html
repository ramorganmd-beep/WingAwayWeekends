Leavenworth

A Bavarian village in the Cascades.

Leavenworth reinvented itself in the 1960s as a Bavarian village, and today its alpine storefronts, beer halls and painted balconies sit beneath dramatic Cascade peaks. Land at Pangborn Memorial in Wenatchee, drive 35 minutes up the Wenatchee River, stay at the Sleeping Lady mountain resort or the Posthotel spa, eat schnitzel at Andreas Keller, hike along Icicle Creek, and come back in December for the Christmas lighting.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Wenatchee.

KEAT, Pangborn Memorial, is a non-towered airport at 1,249 feet with a 7,000-foot runway in East Wenatchee, about 35 minutes from Leavenworth.

The FBO

Pangborn Aviation Services

Pangborn Memorial Airport, East Wenatchee, WA

509-886-0233

Glider activity from March to November. Cashmere-Dryden (8S2), closer to Leavenworth, has only an 1,800-foot runway and no fuel. No live fuel price is shown.

Inland Northwest flying: hot, dry summers with afternoon winds and wildfire smoke, cold winters with fog and low ceilings in the valleys, and the Cascades to the west with icing in winter clouds. About 2030 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Rental car.

A car is needed from Wenatchee; the village itself is walkable.
