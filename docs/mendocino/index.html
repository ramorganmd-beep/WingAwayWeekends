Mendocino Coast

A Victorian village on the headlands.

Mendocino is a New England–style village of water towers and Victorian houses on bluffs above the Pacific, and the Little River airport is just three miles away. Land, stay at a historic inn overlooking the ocean, walk the headlands at sunset, eat at Café Beaujolais, ride the Skunk Train into the redwoods from Fort Bragg and visit the Point Cabrillo lighthouse and the botanical gardens.

01 / Aviation & getting around

A small county field near town.

KLLR, Little River, is a non-towered Mendocino County airport at 574 feet with a 5,249-foot runway, about three miles south of Mendocino village.

The FBO

Little River Airport (Mendocino County)

Little River Airport, Little River, CA

707-367-1137

Fog is the main challenge; have an inland alternate such as Ukiah. Trees on the Runway 11 approach; the threshold is displaced. Call the airport attendant to arrange parking and a ride; 100LL only. (FAA chart supplement remarks.) No live fuel price is shown.

Mendocino coast: fog and low ceilings are common, especially in summer mornings and evenings; clear days are most likely in fall. Have an inland alternate such as Ukiah. About 2170 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Inn shuttle or car.

Mendocino village is walkable; a car helps for Fort Bragg and the redwoods. Ask your inn about airport pickup.
