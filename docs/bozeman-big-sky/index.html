Bozeman & Big Sky

A mountain town on the way to Yellowstone.

Bozeman has grown into Montana’s liveliest small city, with a walkable Main Street, good restaurants and mountains on three sides. Big Sky is an hour south, and Yellowstone’s north and west entrances are within reach. Land at Bozeman Yellowstone International, stay downtown or at Big Sky, see the T. rex at the Museum of the Rockies, soak at Chico Hot Springs and drive into Yellowstone.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Bozeman Yellowstone International.

KBZN, Bozeman Yellowstone International, is a towered field at 4,473 feet with an 8,994-foot main runway in the Gallatin Valley, about 15 minutes from downtown.

The FBO

Signature Aviation Bozeman

Bozeman Yellowstone International Airport, Belgrade, MT

406-388-4152

Busy with airline and jet traffic in ski season and summer. Mountains on all sides; check winds and density altitude. No live fuel price is shown.

Northern Rockies flying: mountain wave and strong winds over the ranges, afternoon thunderstorms and wildfire smoke in summer, early snow from October, and long distances between alternates. Consider a mountain-flying course before you go. About 1640 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Rental car or shuttle.

Downtown Bozeman is walkable; a car or shuttle is needed for Big Sky, the hot springs and Yellowstone.
