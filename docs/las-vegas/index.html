Las Vegas

Shows, steakhouses and desert neon.

Las Vegas is the country’s great over-the-top weekend: resorts the size of towns, headline shows, the Sphere, celebrity restaurants and the Bellagio fountains, with the desert waiting just beyond. Land at Henderson Executive, a calm general-aviation airport south of the Strip, stay at the Wynn or the Venetian, see a show, visit the Neon Museum’s old signs downtown and drive out to Valley of Fire’s red sandstone.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Henderson Executive.

KHND, Henderson Executive, is a towered general-aviation airport at 2,491 feet with a 6,501-foot main runway, about 15 minutes from the south end of the Strip.

The FBO

Henderson Executive Airport FBO (Clark County)

Henderson Executive Airport, Henderson, NV

702-261-4806

Under the Las Vegas Class B; brief the VFR routes. North Las Vegas (KVGT) is another general-aviation option. Summer density altitude is high. No live fuel price is shown.

Desert Southwest flying: summer temperatures above 110°F with very high density altitude, strong afternoon winds and turbulence, monsoon storms from July to September, and long distances between airports. About 1780 nm from Myrtle Beach: two long days each way with several fuel stops. Plan a week, or overnight on the way out and back.

Getting around

Rideshare or car.

Rideshare is easiest on the Strip; rent a car for Valley of Fire and Hoover Dam.
