Minneapolis & St. Paul

Two river cities with art, theater and great food.

Minneapolis and St. Paul face each other across the Mississippi. Stay at the grand Saint Paul Hotel, see the spoon and cherry at the Walker Art Center’s sculpture garden, walk to Minnehaha Falls and the Stone Arch Bridge, eat Indigenous cooking at Owamni and a molten-cheese Jucy Lucy at Matt’s Bar.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at St. Paul Downtown.

KSTP, St. Paul Downtown Holman Field, is a towered GA airport on the Mississippi, about 5 minutes from downtown St. Paul and 20 from Minneapolis.

The FBO

Signature Aviation

St. Paul Downtown Airport, St. Paul, MN

651-224-1100

St. Paul Flight Center is a second FBO. Confirm hours, fees and cars. No live fuel price is shown.

Upper Midwest weather: summer thunderstorms, fast-moving fronts, and cold, snowy winters with icing. About 940 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.

Seaplane access

Wipline (09Y), public-use seaplane base

Inver Grove Heights, MN · 612-286-6612

612-286-6612

Water lanes: 17W/35W 8,000 × 500 ft on the Mississippi River, about 8 nm south of downtown St. Paul. 100LL and Jet A; call the manager for fuel.

River channel marked by buoys; floating debris on the river at all times. (FAA chart supplement remarks.)

Home of Wipaire, the float maker. You will need a ride into the city.

Water landing rules, conditions and docking change; call the base and check the chart supplement before you go.

Getting around

Rideshare.

Downtown St. Paul is walkable; rideshare or the light rail to Minneapolis.
