Fort Lauderdale

Beaches, canals and the yachting capital, with a GA-friendly airport.

Fort Lauderdale Executive is one of the country’s busiest general aviation airports, about 20 minutes from the beach. Stay on the sand or on Las Olas Boulevard, ride the water taxi along the canals, tour the Bonnet House estate and eat waterfront seafood on the Intracoastal.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Fort Lauderdale Executive.

KFXE, Fort Lauderdale Executive, is a busy towered general aviation airport with a 6,002 ft runway (09/27), north of downtown and under the Miami Class B and Fort Lauderdale airspace. Several FBOs serve the field; Banyan Air Service is open 24 hours.

The FBO

Banyan Air Service

5360 NW 20th Terrace, Fort Lauderdale, FL · open 24 hours

Busy South Florida airspace: brief the Miami Class B, the Fort Lauderdale Class C next door and the arrival procedures.

Getting around

Uber and the water taxi.

Uber to the beach or Las Olas, about 20 minutes. The water taxi runs along the Intracoastal and New River between the beach, Las Olas and the hotels.
