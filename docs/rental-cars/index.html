Getting into town

Rental cars at the FBO

Most weekend trips need a car. With a little notice, one can be waiting at the FBO when you land.

Four steps

  1. Call the FBO first. Ask which rental companies deliver there, and whether a crew car is available for short trips.
  2. Book through an aviation program (below), not the regular airline-terminal counter.
  3. Give the airport, the FBO’s name and your arrival time, plus a cell number.
  4. Confirm with the FBO the day before, especially for weekend or late arrivals.

One thing to check: a rental company serving an airport doesn’t always deliver to every FBO on that field. Confirm delivery with your FBO before you count on the car.

Aviation rental programs

Enterprise and National

A shared General Aviation & FBO program. Send a reservation request online and they email a confirmation within 24 hours. Delivery requires National’s Emerald Club membership, delivery rates include extra costs, and some locations are excluded.

Avis and others

Some FBOs run their own Avis, Budget or local rental counter. Your FBO will know what’s on the field.

Already use FlightBridge? Its free app can book FBO-delivered rental cars and hotels for your trip.

AOPA members can get a rental discount through AOPA’s car rental program. Programs, prices and rules change; check the company’s own page when you book.

Crew cars

Many FBOs keep a courtesy or crew car for short trips, such as lunch in town. They’re first come, first served and meant to come back within a couple of hours, not overnight. Call ahead, bring it back on time with a little gas in it, and buying fuel at that FBO is good manners.
