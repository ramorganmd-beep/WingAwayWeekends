Pittsburgh

Rivers, bridges, museums and the best city view in the East.

Pittsburgh surprises first-time visitors. Ride the 1877 Duquesne Incline to Mount Washington for the view of the rivers and bridges, spend a day at the Andy Warhol Museum and the Carnegie museums, wander the Phipps Conservatory, graze the Strip District and finish with dinner overlooking the skyline.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Allegheny County.

KAGC, Allegheny County Airport, is a towered GA airport about 20 minutes from downtown, with a classic 1931 terminal.

The FBO

Vantage Aviation

Allegheny County Airport, West Mifflin, PA

412-469-6800

Atlantic Aviation is a second FBO. Confirm hours, fees and cars. No live fuel price is shown.

Appalachian weather: valley fog in the mornings, ridge-top turbulence, summer thunderstorms, and low ceilings and icing in winter. About 400 nm from Myrtle Beach.

Getting around

Rideshare.

Downtown is walkable; rideshare to the museums and Mount Washington.
