Fort Worth & the Stockyards

Cowtown: longhorns, steaks and great art.

Fort Worth is where the West begins. Watch longhorns parade down Exchange Avenue in the Stockyards’ daily cattle drive, two-step at Billy Bob’s, eat a steak at a 1940s steakhouse, then spend a morning at the Kimbell and Amon Carter art museums. Pilots can start at the Vintage Flying Museum right on the airfield.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land five minutes from the Stockyards.

KFTW, Fort Worth Meacham International, is a towered airport just north of downtown, about five minutes from the Stockyards.

The FBO

Texas Jet

200 Texas Way, Fort Worth, TX 76106

817-624-8438

Texas Jet is open 24 hours. Ask about rental cars, parking and fees. No live fuel price is shown.

Meacham sits under the Dallas–Fort Worth Class B airspace, among the busiest in the country. Brief your arrival carefully. About 930 nm from Myrtle Beach: plan a fuel stop and three nights. Review the sectional and current NOTAMs.

Getting around

Rideshare, then walk.

The Stockyards are walkable. The museums and downtown are a short ride away.
