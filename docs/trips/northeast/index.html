The Northeast in a week

Seven nights by air: Cape May’s Victorian beach town, Newport’s mansions, Nantucket, Acadia and Bar Harbor, then Stowe in the Green Mountains.

    Before you go

    Build in a weather day. Northeast summers bring afternoon thunderstorms and coastal fog; plan at least one spare day on a week-long trip, and fly the long legs in the morning.

    These are planning estimates. Distances are straight lines, times assume no wind, and fuel-stop flags use your aircraft profile with its reserve. Check weather, NOTAMs, the chart supplement, airspace and performance for every leg. Nothing here is booked or confirmed.
