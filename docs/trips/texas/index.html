Texas Hill Country and the Gulf

Seven nights in Texas: Galveston’s Victorian Strand, Round Top’s antiques, Fredericksburg’s wineries, a fly-in resort at Horseshoe Bay and San Antonio’s River Walk.

    Before you go

    Build in a weather day. Spring brings wildflowers and severe storms; summer afternoons are hot and bumpy. Fly early, watch for morning stratus off the Gulf and brief the Houston and San Antonio airspace.
