Granbury

A courthouse square on the lake.

Granbury is an easy Texas weekend southwest of Fort Worth, with one of the state’s prettiest courthouse squares lined with 1880s limestone buildings, a working opera house and a lake at the end of the street. Land at Granbury Regional, stay on the square at the Nutt House Hotel or at the Inn on Lake Granbury, catch a show at the Granbury Opera House, and drive 20 minutes to Dinosaur Valley State Park, where dinosaur tracks are preserved in the Paluxy River bed.

01 / Aviation & getting around

Land at Granbury Regional.

KGDJ, Granbury Regional, is a non-towered city airport at 835 feet with a 5,201-foot runway, about 10 minutes from the square.

The FBO

Granbury Regional Airport (city FBO)

Granbury Regional Airport, Granbury, TX

817-579-8533

Just outside the Dallas–Fort Worth Class B; brief the airspace. No live fuel price is shown.

Texas flying: hot, bumpy summer afternoons with pop-up thunderstorms; spring severe weather (hail, tornadoes and squall lines) from March to June; gusty south winds; and low morning stratus that spreads inland from the Gulf. About 950 nm from Myrtle Beach: a long cross-country day with two fuel stops. Plan four or more nights, or break the trip with an overnight on the way.
