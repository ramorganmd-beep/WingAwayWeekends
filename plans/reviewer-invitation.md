## 1. Personal email (a pilot or friend you know)

**Subject:** Would you review a fly-in weekend guide for [Destination]?

Hi [Name],

I've been building WingAway Weekends (wingawayweekends.com), a free site for pilots that plans two-night getaways for a pilot and a partner. Each guide covers the airport and FBO, where to stay and eat, what to do and a sample Friday–Sunday itinerary.

You know [Destination] better than I do, and I'd love your eyes on the guide before I share it more widely:
[guide link, e.g. wingawayweekends.com/charlottesville/]

It takes about an hour. The attached one-page checklist shows what to look for: anything closed, out of date or missing, and the places you'd send a friend. You don't need to visit anything new.

Send changes to hello@wingawayweekends.com, or just reply here. If it all looks right, a reply of "lg" is enough.

With your permission, the guide will say "Reviewed by [your name]," and you'll get an early look at new destinations in your area.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Your aircraft / home airport, optional]
