WingAway Weekends · Reviewer checklist

For pilots who know a destination well · wingawayweekends.com · About an hour per guide

Thank you for helping. Each guide is a two-night getaway plan for a pilot and a partner: the airport and FBO, where to stay and eat, what to do and a sample itinerary. You don’t need to visit anything new. Just tell us what’s right, what’s out of date and what you’d recommend instead.

Guide:     Reviewer:     Date:  

1 · Flying in

  • The airport is the one you’d choose (or suggest a better one)
  • FBO name, address and phone are correct
  • Courtesy car, rental and ground-transport notes are accurate
  • Airspace, terrain and weather cautions are right and complete
  • Fees, hours or parking quirks pilots should know

2 · Where to stay

  • Each hotel or inn is open and still a good pick for couples
  • The description and category fit (luxury, inn, resort)
  • A better or newer place we should list instead

3 · Where to eat

  • Each restaurant is open and still worth the trip
  • Days, reservations and dress notes are right
  • A favorite we left out

4 · Things to do

  • Each item is open, current and worth the time
  • Drive times and distances look realistic
  • Anything seasonal, closed or under repair

5 · Spa, wine, shopping, events

  • Spas, wineries, breweries and shops are current
  • Event months and seasons are right

6 · Itinerary

  • The Friday–Sunday plan is realistic and relaxed
  • The order of stops makes sense geographically

7 · Links and photo

  • Each “Official details” link opens the place’s own website (not a booking reseller)
  • Send official links for any place that shows “Website & map”
  • The photo really looks like the destination

8 · Families

  • Would this weekend work with kids? Yes / No / With changes
How to send your review
  1. Email hello@wingawayweekends.com with the guide name in the subject.
  2. List each change: the place, what’s wrong and the fix (paste official website links).
  3. When everything looks right, just reply “lg” (looks good).
Your credit: with your permission, the guide will say “Reviewed by [your name],” and you’ll get an early look at new destinations in your area.

Guidelines: suggest places you’d send a friend; skip chains unless they’re the best option; flag closures even if unsure. Flight planning stays with the pilot. The guides are discovery aids, not flight plans.
